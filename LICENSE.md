# Sources, License & Free-Sharing Terms

This repository organizes and packages the user-contributed "Code Animation — 341 Style Prompts — Free" material pack. The material lives in [`skills/code-animation-prompts/assets/library/`](skills/code-animation-prompts/assets/library), keeping the original prompts, examples, notes, and licenses; the picker, stats, and new files modified to integrate community material are registered separately in the skill's [change list](skills/code-animation-prompts/references/library-changes.json). The original package's SHA-256 baseline is kept in [source-manifest.json](docs/source-manifest.json).

## The original material's sharing terms

The original [`about.md`](skills/code-animation-prompts/assets/library/about.md) states:

> All free — no DMs, no paid course needed.
>
> You may use it for free and share it with others for free; when you share it, please share it for free too.
>
> Do not package this content as a course, a paid skill, or anything else you sell for money.

This repository shares the collection for free and does not sell it as a paid skill. The added skill packaging, scripts, and showcase docs are provided free with the collection and may be freely used, modified, and shared as long as the source and this notice are kept; do not bundle this collection for paid sale.
