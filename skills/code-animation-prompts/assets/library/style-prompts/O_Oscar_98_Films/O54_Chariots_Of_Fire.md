# O54 Chariots of Fire

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Blockbuster Era 1980–1989
- What it looks like: A four-colour lithograph after Jean Droit's 1924 Paris Olympics poster: classical nudes, limited inks, clumped litho grain, uneven registration. Chariots of Fire is about the 1924 Olympics — the beach run looks like that poster coming alive.
- Style: 1924 Paris Olympics lithograph poster
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1981

## Style Prompt

1920s stone lithograph poster in four inks: sea blue #245A8C, ochre #D49640, deep brown #40271B, warm red #E47E62, on aged cream #F1E7D1, misregistered about 1/700 of frame width. Every tone is ink grain, never a flat fill: specks about 1/1000 of frame width in soft clumps, covering 20–40% of pale areas, 50–80% of mid-tones, nearly all of solids (with pinholes); no stray specks on bare paper. Roles: blue for sky, water, cool shadow; ochre for ground and skin; brown for outlines, darks, clothes; red sparingly. Values calm and pale. Classical figures: ochre lit side, brown core-shadow line down each limb, head 1/8 of body; groups in a row. Slow motion carried by spray, dust, hair, cloth. Camera: long-lens front views, ground-level shots; optional serif title band. Reference: Jean Droit, 1924 Paris Olympics poster. Avoid: coarse speckle, pictogram figures, vector fills.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

### Director's notes

Clawd runs in the pack: a white headband, race number 98 pinned to its chest. The running is at 1×, so it reads as the original film's slow motion. Four hard cuts: faces, feet, Liddell's head-back running pose, the whole pack in profile; in the last shot the poster lettering is printed over the frame, turning the scene into a 1924 poster.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A long-lens head-on shot: the pack of runners coming along the water's edge toward the camera, Clawd among them in its white headband with race number 98; the camera pulls back slightly. (0.7s)
2. A low close-up at ground level: bare feet splashing into the shallows, reflections on the wet sand; the camera slides sideways. (0.6s)
3. Close-up of Liddell: head back, mouth open, Abrahams behind him; the camera pulls back slightly. (0.5s)
4. The whole pack in profile, Clawd in the middle; as the camera pulls back, the poster lettering 'JEUX DE LA VIIIe OLYMPIADE · PARIS 1924' is printed over the frame. (0.8s)
