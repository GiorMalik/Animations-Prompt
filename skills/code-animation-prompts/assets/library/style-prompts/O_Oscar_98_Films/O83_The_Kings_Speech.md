## Style Prompt

Painterly interiors where the wall carries the image (outdoor subjects move into a related room with a window). Primary: a diamond-tufted padded wall in gold #C9A050 over deep brown #3A2610, brass button highlights, a warm glow behind the sound source (e.g. a hanging microphone). Secondary, for quiet scenes: a cool grey-green wall worn through to blue, pink, ochre and plaster, with damp lines and fine cracks; patches irregular, under a third of the wall. Clean-edged shapes under a subtle brush texture (strokes ~1/120 of frame width, ±5% brightness); faces, small objects crisp. Sound: 4–8 thin gold oscilloscope lines crossing the wall, spiky, later smooth. Figures in a corner, lots of wall. Camera: slow push-ins, one close-up of the source. References: cinematographer Danny Cohen, designer Eve Stewart (*The King's Speech*, 2010). Avoid: centred symmetry; coarse dabbed strokes; map-like peel; neon-bright waves.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Digital oil-paint brushwork, layer upon layer of peeling paint, 1930s broadcast room, thin gold sound waves

### Director's notes

Clawd plays George VI: a small crown, wing collar and dark tie, a cigarette in the consulting room, a typed script in the broadcast room. Three hard cuts tell the whole therapy: huddled in the corner, the sound wave going from stammered to flowing, then Logue keeping time as the speech goes out.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Wide: Logue's consulting room, Clawd huddled at the left end of the old leather sofa with a cigarette, a large expanse of peeling wall above, a gramophone with a horn at the right; a slow push. (0.8s)
2. Extreme close-up: the marble microphone in its spring suspension, Clawd's crown and eyes at the left; the gold sound waves start broken (the stammer), then flow smoothly; a slow push. (0.9s)
3. Medium wide: the 1939 broadcast room, Clawd with its script at the left, the microphone in the centre, Logue at the right keeping time with his hand, the ON AIR light coming on; a slight pull. (0.9s)
