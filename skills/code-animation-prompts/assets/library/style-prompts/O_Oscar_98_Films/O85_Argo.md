# O85 Argo

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Indie Era 2010–2019
- What it looks like: A 1970s Jack Kirby comic spread out on a producer's desk: thick ink lines, Kirby energy bubbles, four-colour Ben-Day dots on yellowed newsprint. The film's fake sci-fi movie used Kirby's own concept art, so the comic flips from the fake epic all the way to the real runway escape.
- Style: Jack Kirby 1970s comic page
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2012

## Style Prompt

1970s Jack Kirby comic page. Heavy tapered brush contours, feathered spot blacks, exaggerated perspective, Kirby Krackle energy bubbles, block sound effects, hand-lettered balloons and yellow captions. CMYK flats printed as 45° Ben-Day dots: cyan #1696CD, magenta #DE2C70, yellow #FFD616, black #1C161A on aged newsprint #EEE2C2, colour plates 1 px off register. Paper grain, browned edges, a gutter fold shadow, warm lamp falloff. Lay the story out in panels and move between them: push into the splash, pan along a strip. Dots stay round and grow into pop-art dots on push-ins. Avoid: smooth digital gradients, clean white paper, stiff tube-like machines.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays the comic-book space hero: a red-lined high collar, a blue cape, gold wristbands, a Kirby-style light gun. The beam fires within the first beat, then two hard cuts flip the pages from the fake epic to the real escape; finally Clawd sheds the costume and becomes a passenger behind a porthole. 2.5 seconds, three panels, like reading a comic.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A medium shot of the cosmic double-page spread, slowly pushing in: Clawd the caped space hero standing on a rock ledge firing its light gun, the beam blowing up a robot's reactor, 'KRAKA-THOOM'. (0.9s)
2. A hard cut to the red filmstrip panel: the hostages in the terminal and the narration 'THE MOVIE WAS FAKE. THE MISSION WAS REAL.', the camera moving slightly right. (0.8s)
3. A hard cut to the steel-blue night-runway panel, panning left to right past the pursuing trucks and jeeps to the airliner lifting off; in a small porthole panel at the corner, Clawd sitting behind the window looking back. (0.8s)
