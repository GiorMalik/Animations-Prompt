# O12 Gone with the Wind

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Colour & War 1939–1949
- What it looks like: Impasto oil painting: palette-knife blocks of colour, ridges of piled paint, the heat of Technicolor — a sky burning from gold to blood red, red earth, black silhouettes. Colour arrives for the whole series with *Gone with the Wind*; the first full-colour frame is Tara at sunset.
- Style: Technicolor impasto oil painting
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1939

## Style Prompt

Impasto oil in a 1939 Technicolor palette. Palette-knife slabs: long horizontal sky bands (1/15-1/6 of frame width long, about 1/60 tall), shorter strokes following form on land and figures, calm thin areas between; ridges lit from upper left, paint thickest in highlights, canvas weave where thin. Default sunset sky: wine #3A0912, red #A8241A, orange #F27A22, gold #FFD878; ground red-brown #6E2214; warm near-black silhouettes. The hero the story centres on stays a readable warm mid-tone (e.g. #D97757), never a silhouette. Strong backlight, gold rims; in close shots a warm side light on half the face and hands. Epic-poster composition: big dark silhouettes (trees, buildings, rocks, figures) on a low horizon. Lamps as painted flames. Camera: slow push on the wide, low push-in on the hero, crane up to a vista. Avoid: tiled same-size strokes, muddy browns, smooth gradients.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Scarlett: a wide-brimmed straw hat with a green ribbon, a radish in hand. For the first 7 beats the sky burns from gold to blood red; on beat 6 it raises the radish. Four hard cuts walk through Tara, the vow, Atlanta and the station, and the last 5 beats are one rising crane shot.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Wide silhouette of Tara: the great oak, the white house and cedars standing black against a sky burning from gold to blood red, Clawd standing on a red-earth mound holding a radish; a slow push. (1.7s)
2. Pushing in low over the horizon, a burning sky behind it: Clawd in the green-ribboned wide-brimmed straw hat raises the radish high, backlit with a gold rim. (1s)
3. Close-up of Clawd's face and straw hat, the ribbon tails fluttering in the wind. (0.4s)
4. The burning of Atlanta: a carriage charges through burning warehouses and timber frames, Clawd sitting on the box beside Rhett, Prissy and Melanie behind; the camera tracks sideways, flames licking across the foreground. (1.5s)
5. One rising crane shot at the station: climbing and pulling back from Clawd standing among the wounded, row after row of wounded men spreading across the red earth to the horizon, a tattered flag in the middle of the field, Clawd left as a single orange dot. (2.1s)
