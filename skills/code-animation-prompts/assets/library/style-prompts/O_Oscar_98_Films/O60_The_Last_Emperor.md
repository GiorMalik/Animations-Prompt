# O60 The Last Emperor

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Blockbuster Era 1980–1989
- What it looks like: Iron-wire lines on silk with layered renderings of azurite blue, malachite green, cinnabar and gold, palace architecture in ruled-line perspective, thousands of kneeling officials. The little emperor's enthronement is a Qing court ceremony, so it is painted the way Qing court painters recorded ceremonies.
- Style: Gongbi heavy colour · silk court painting
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1987

## Style Prompt

Chinese gongbi zhongcai (fine-line, heavy-colour court painting) on aged silk. Far to near, each form: flat base, a wash darkening its lower, overlapped edges, an even iron-wire outline (dark brown, ~1/900 frame width), gold and white lifts. Last, a 30% warm ochre glaze over everything, lines still clear. Minerals: cinnabar #C23A22, azurite #2C5A9A, malachite #3A8A6A, gamboge #E8B830, gold #CAA04A, muted by the glaze; ground and sky mid-value warm silk ochre, never near-white, saturated blue or orange; time of day shows only as overall warmth. No cast shadows or glare. Fine-line water ripples. Ruled-line architecture, every tile drawn; crowds all differ; a lone figure works. Faint silk weave (±3%), brown vignette. Symmetrical one-point composition: a large figure before a deep, ordered space. Slow pushes. Reference: Qing court scrolls, e.g. *Kangxi's Southern Inspection Tour*. Avoid: copy-pasted figures; unwashed flat fills; loose ink wash; poster saturation.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Gongbi heavy colour, ruled-line architecture, Qing court scrolls, Storaro's colour

### Director's notes

Clawd plays the young Puyi: a red-tasselled court hat, a court necklace, horseshoe sleeves. The curtains part and Clawd walks out on the beat; a hard cut to the wave of officials kowtowing, and the last beats land on the poster-style wide. The scene plays at 1.1×.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A yellow silk curtain embroidered with coiled dragons fills the frame; the curtain parts and Clawd in its red-tasselled court hat walks up the steps; the camera pulls back slightly. (1s)
2. A hard cut to a low tracking shot sweeping across the kneeling officials, rows of robed mandarins bowing in a wave. (0.6s)
3. A hard cut to the poster composition, slowly widening: Clawd large on the steps, behind it the square full of kneeling officials and the golden double-eaved roof, a cricket jar in the foreground. (0.9s)
