# O7 It Happened One Night

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Silent & Early Sound 1927–1938
- What it looks like: A 1934 black-and-white rubber-hose cartoon: ink lines that swell and thin, pie-cut eyes, white gloves, cel paint laid over grey watercolour backgrounds, with film grain. From the same year as the film, and a road comedy's jokes suit a cartoon's rhythm.
- Style: 1930s B&W rubber-hose cartoon
- Keywords: 2D·B&W
- Year: 1934

## Style Prompt

Early-1930s black-and-white rubber-hose cartoon. Characters: noodle limbs, white gloves, pie-cut eyes, round heads; thick-thin ink #141414 (1/400-1/150 of frame width) swelling away from the light (default upper left); fills pure black and white plus at most one light grey for the hard shadow, gloss on blacks. Backgrounds stay mid-grey (30-80%), never pure black/white: graphite lines left in, grey watercolour washes with pooled edges and granulation, paper grain. Only the hero the story centres on has colour (e.g. #D97757): its whole body, or its one key prop if the hero is human. ±1 px gate weave, grain (±4%), flicker, scratches, dust. Actions anticipate, overshoot, settle: squash on landing, stretch on fast moves, eyes pop when surprised, a camera jolt on hits. References: Fleischer's Betty Boop shorts; Disney's Silly Symphonies backgrounds. Avoid: smooth vector lines, grey characters merging with the background.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Peter: grey fedora, loosened striped tie, a small moustache, white gloves at the ends of its arms. On beats 3–4 Ellie lifts her skirt; on beat 5 the car brakes hard (the only moment played back at 1× speed), and Clawd's dropped jaw lands exactly on it. Two hard cuts: thumb → the two of them → the finish.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Clawd's raised white-gloved thumb and smug face, an empty road behind it, a car coming over the hilltop; a slow push. (0.7s)
2. Two-shot: Clawd thumbing at the roadside, Ellie sitting on a fence lifting her skirt to her calves; a gentle pull. (0.7s)
3. Closing wide: the car brakes hard between the two of them, the driver's eyes popping out; Clawd stares wide-eyed, arms spread, its hat jumping; the frame jolts on the hard stop. (1.1s)
