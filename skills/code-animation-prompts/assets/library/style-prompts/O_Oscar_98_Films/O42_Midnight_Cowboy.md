# O42 Midnight Cowboy

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Swinging Sixties 1960–1969
- What it looks like: Warhol's photo silkscreen: a black halftone photo layer printed unevenly, misregistered over loose flat colour blocks, on a silver ground, the image repeated twice, three times. The film has a Warhol Factory-style party in it, and the Elvis he painted is a cowboy.
- Style: Warhol photo silkscreen · Double Elvis
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1969

## Style Prompt

Andy Warhol photo-silkscreen. Two plates: a high-contrast black halftone photograph at 45°, dots about 1/200 of frame width, few mid-tones; and hand-painted flat colour blocks underneath, offset about 1/100 of frame width with wobbly edges: taxi yellow #F3BE14, skin pink #F2A197, cream #EFE6CF, red #E2462B, on brushed silver paint #BABCC0 with a soft sheen. The one hero subject takes the strongest block colour. Uneven squeegee pressure: dots flood into blotches in places, drag into dry horizontal streaks and pinholes elsewhere, over faint canvas weave. Repetition is the composition: the same figure or object, full-length, printed side by side two then three times, slightly overlapping, on empty silver. Settings appear only as drained-to-silver prints in the repeat. Camera: handheld shake, a push in, flat frontal tableaux. Reference: Andy Warhol, *Double Elvis* (1963). Avoid: clean even halftone, perfect registration, uniform crispness.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Photo silkscreen, Warhol's Double Elvis (1963), silver paint ground, offset colour blocks

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Joe Buck: a black Stetson, a red neckerchief, suede fringes on its arms. First the film's line 'I'm walkin' here!': the taxi brakes hard, it jumps back, Ratso slaps the bonnet and shouts. Then the picture turns into a Warhol silkscreen and Clawd strikes Elvis's gunslinger pose — printed twice, then three times.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A Sixth Avenue crosswalk: a yellow taxi brakes hard and rushes in, Clawd as Joe Buck jumping back, eyes glaring, Ratso slapping the bonnet once; handheld shake, a slow push in. (1.1s)
2. Close-up of Ratso: cigarette in its mouth, shouting at the taxi and pointing at the driver (I'm walkin' here!); pushing in. (0.6s)
3. The street scene fades to silver and is printed as a Warhol Double: two Clawds in Stetsons holding revolvers, overlapping, then three; a flat-on frozen frame. (0.8s)
