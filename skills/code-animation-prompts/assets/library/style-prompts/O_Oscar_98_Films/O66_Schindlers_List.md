# O66 Schindler's List

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Fin de Siècle 1990–1999
- What it looks like: Cold grey, high-contrast black and white, coarse grain, highlights blooming; only the little girl's red coat and Clawd's orange have colour. Exactly Spielberg's device: the red coat and the Sabbath candle flames are the film's only colour.
- Style: B&W film · the only red coat
- Keywords: 2D·B&W
- Year: 1993

## Style Prompt

Black-and-white film look: cold neutral greys, a strong S-curve (blacks near #111, whites near #EEE), highlights haloing in haze, a soft old lens, silver grain re-rolled every frame (±6%), slight flicker, rare dust, vignette. Everything is grey except the one hero the story centres on, saturated, mainly clothing (e.g. terracotta #D97757); optionally one red story object (#A3241F–#C9463C). Lamps and fires stay grey, unless a flame turning from grey to warm is the story beat. Long-lens compression, soft foreground branches; in a crowd, no two people alike in build, tone or gait. Default: overcast mid-key, shadows open. Signature shot: high long-lens view of many small walking figures, the coloured one among them; without a crowd, the hero tiny in wide grey space. Reference: cinematographer Janusz Kamiński (*Schindler's List*, 1993). Avoid: sepia or blue toning; grain-free greys; more than two coloured things.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

High-contrast B&W film, coarse silver grain, selective colour, Janusz Kamiński

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Schindler: a grey fedora, hands on the reins, sitting on horseback looking down. Four hard cuts follow his gaze: the hillside, the long-lens POV, the street, ending on the candles, each shot about a beat and a half. Restrained throughout: people are just walking, and no violence appears.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Clawd as Schindler on a dark horse on the hillside, the street below packed with people being herded, a red dot among the crowd; a gentle push. (0.7s)
2. A hard cut to Schindler's long-lens POV, holding on the little girl in the red coat walking alone through a gap in the crowd. (0.6s)
3. A hard cut to the street: a tracking shot moving at the girl's speed, three rows of people walking left past the shops, an out-of-focus coat sweeping across the lens. (0.6s)
4. A hard cut to two Sabbath candles in the dark: the flames first burning grey-white, then turning to colour; a slow push. Clawd is not in the frame. (0.6s)
