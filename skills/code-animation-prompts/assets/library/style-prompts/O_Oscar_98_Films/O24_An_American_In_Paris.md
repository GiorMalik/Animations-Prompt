# O24 An American in Paris

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Widescreen 1950–1959
- What it looks like: Raoul Dufy's watercolour: quick blue-black ink lines, with transparent blocks of cobalt blue, lemon yellow and rose red underneath, line and colour never lining up. The film's closing ballet was designed after Dufy, and the Place de la Concorde and the dancers are painted his way.
- Style: Dufy watercolour ballet
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1951

## Style Prompt

Paint in Raoul Dufy's watercolour manner: colour first, line second, never aligned. Transparent washes (wet edges, pooled rims, granulation) cover most of the frame, 10-40% oversized, slid one way by about 1/30 of frame width: cobalt #2258CE for sky and water, lemon #FCE02E for light and ground, rose #EC3A7C for warm planes. Overlaps mix violet or green, never brown; keep the hero's wash off blue. Then draw the whole scene densely in quick, thin blue-black ink (about 1/700 of frame width) with flicked ends: buildings, windows, figures, extras; straight edges overshoot corners; trees and clouds as looped strings. Ink #1E1E3A; bare paper 20-30%, skin included. The hero the story centres on gets its own wash (e.g. #D97757). Figures bounce. Camera: small pushes, cuts on the beat (or every 2 s). Avoid: muddy colour, lines tracing the colour, sparse line.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Loose ink lines + offset colour blocks, Raoul Dufy, Minnelli's ballet sets, cold-press watercolour paper

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Jerry from the poster: a cobalt-blue beret tipped back (Gene Kelly's), a small rose-red neckerchief, a brush flinging a ribbon of paint. In all three shots it dances on every beat, and the dance steps are the cutting rhythm: the square, the close-up with Lise, the flag-filled street. The performance runs at 1.8×.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Wide of the Place de la Concorde: dancers spinning around the fountain, the obelisk and the Eiffel Tower behind; Clawd as Jerry in a cobalt cap with a brush dances beside Lise in her yellow dress; the camera pushes in slightly. (1s)
2. Close-up of Clawd jumping in mid-air, its brush flinging a ribbon of paint, Lise's arm reaching into the frame; a slow push. (0.8s)
3. Cut to a Paris street hung with tricolours, facades in pink, yellow and blue, Clawd bouncing between pedestrians in the middle of the street; a slow push. (0.8s)
