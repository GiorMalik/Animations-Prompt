# O94 CODA

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Right Now 2020–2025
- What it looks like: A polished sperm-whale tooth on an oak table, engraved with fine black lines and touched with red, blue and yellow: the Gloucester fishing boat, the family hauling in the nets, Ruby singing at the bow. This is the folk craft of the film's own New England coast.
- Style: Scrimshaw whale-tooth carving
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2021

## Style Prompt

Scrimshaw: 19th-century sailor's engraving on a whale tooth. Signature shot: a broad, flat-sided tooth lies horizontal on an oak table, about 85% of frame width and 70% of its height; ink bottle, jackknife beside. Engrave in near-black #17110c: needle hatching (lines 1/800 of frame width, spaced about 1/500), swelling outlines, stipple. Lines cover ~70% of the face, dense in sea, subject and land, sky lightly hatched; seas as rows of stylised waves. The hero is large (~1/3 of the tooth's height), coloured; otherwise red #9a2a22, blue #2b4a78, yellow #e9b526 only as small touches. Warm ivory, growth bands, scratches, honey tip. Folk layout: rope border, ribbon motto, rayed sun, motifs from the subject's world. Shade the edges, one gloss streak; don't warp the picture. Push in, then pan slowly as waves animate. Avoid: thick lines, solid fills, a slim horn.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

19th-century whaling sailors' scrimshaw, knife hatching and stipple, tabletop still-life product lighting

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Ruby, wearing a yellow waterproof fisherman's hat, standing on a fish box at the bow. Only one cut: from the object into the engraving, then one gentle pan, carrying the eye from her father's signing hands to the song, as the staff lines unfurl with the singing.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Wide: the engraved whale tooth lying on the oak table, a pocket knife and ink bottle beside it; the camera pushing in until the tooth fills the frame. (0.8s)
2. Cut into the engraving: a medium wide of the fishing boat, the camera slowly panning left to right and pushing in gently, from Frank hauling the nets while signing 'I love you' to Clawd singing at the bow, a line of sheet music unfurling from its raised arm. (1.7s)
