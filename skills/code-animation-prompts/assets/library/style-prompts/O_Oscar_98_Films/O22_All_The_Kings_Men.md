# O22 All the King's Men

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Colour & War 1939–1949
- What it looks like: A Southern campaign poster's wood-type letterpress: red, blue and black inks on cream paper, wood grain in the solids, uneven inking, misregistered plates, the portrait in halftone dots. This is Stark's campaign rally, with Clawd printed in a fourth orange plate.
- Style: Southern campaign wood-type poster
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1949

## Style Prompt

A letterpress poster from wood type and cuts. Three inks on cream stock #EEE3C7: blue #20488C (sky, water, shadow masses), red #C82C28 (warm surfaces, stripes), black #1E1B1E (outlines, type); only the hero the story centres on gets a spot ink (e.g. #D97757). Each ink 2-4 px off register; solids knock out the inks beneath, halftones overprint. Wood grain, pinholed starved patches, squeezed edges. Mid tones: coarse dots (about 1/200 of frame width), varied angles, on at most a third of the frame. Figures as linocuts: heavy outlines, gouged white lines, crescent shadows. Picture fills about 70%; short wood-type lines (about 1/10 of frame height, user's language) with offset shadows sit on banners and signs. Camera: small pushes, a lateral track, a bump on each strong beat (or every 2 s). Reference: Hatch Show Print. Avoid: busy grey mid-tones, perfect registration.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Wood-type letterpress, Hatch Show Print, linocut figures, halftone portrait

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Willie Stark: a fedora pushed back, a loosened red-and-blue tie, a rolled-up speech in one hand. In the wide its fist is raised halfway; the middle shot is the crowd's response; in the close-up it punches its fist to the top on the beat and swings once more, the camera giving a small jolt as the punch lands. All hard cuts.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Wide of a bunting-draped platform in a town square: Clawd as Stark stands before its own giant halftone portrait, fist raised halfway, the crowd below cheering with STARK FOR GOVERNOR placards; the camera pushes in slightly. (0.9s)
2. Cut into the crowd: placards waving up and down, fists and shouting mouths, EVERY MAN A KING; the camera pans right. (0.7s)
3. Close-up of Clawd, fedora pushed back, tie loosened: its fist punches to the top, then swings once more; the camera jolts with the punch. (0.9s)
