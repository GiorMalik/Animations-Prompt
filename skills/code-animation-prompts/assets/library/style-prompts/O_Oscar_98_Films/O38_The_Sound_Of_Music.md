# O38 The Sound of Music

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Swinging Sixties 1960–1969
- What it looks like: A picture-book 3D procedural Alps: saturated emerald green, cobalt blue and snow white, sculptural ridges, cotton-ball clouds, waves of grass rippling in the wind. Restaging the opening helicopter aerial, ending with Maria spinning on the mountaintop.
- Style: Picture-book 3D Alps aerial
- Keywords: 3D
- Year: 1965

## Style Prompt

Storybook 3D landscape in saturated 1960s widescreen colour: smooth sculpted forms, not low-poly or photoreal. Default light: bright sun high, a little behind the camera: emerald grass #3FA33A, cobalt sky #2F6FD0, clean white snow and clouds. Rounded ridges, snow lines following slope, grey-ochre rock bands, cloud shadows. Haze only on the farthest ridges, about 25% lilac. Cumulus are lit white cotton puffs. Knee-high grass and wildflowers fill the foreground, bending in gusts. A lower sun lengthens shadows and warms only cloud edges; other settings keep these colours. Camera: one continuous move: 0–2 s high flight, 2–4 s descent, then a low orbit through the grass around the subject (about 20° per second, subject about 1/4 of frame height). References: cinematographer Ted McCord (*The Sound of Music*, 1965); Miyazaki's meadows. Avoid: violet wash, needle peaks, grey photoreal rock, static grass.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Procedural 3D terrain (three.js), Todd-AO widescreen colour, Ted McCord's helicopter opening, Miyazaki's meadows

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Maria: an edelweiss on its head and a small knotted headscarf, one hand holding the striped blanket bundle and the other a guitar case, just like the poster. The spin is one continuous curve running through all three shots, each cut landing mid-turn; on the last beat both arms rise high.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. The picture opens out to full screen from the last film's two binocular circles: a high aerial flying through cotton-ball clouds over the snowy peaks. (0.7s)
2. Descending toward the grassy mountaintop: Clawd tiny in the centre of the frame, already spinning with its blanket bundle and guitar case flung wide, forest and field patches far below; the camera drops toward it. (0.8s)
3. A low camera at grass level, circling around Clawd as it completes a full turn, blanket bundle and guitar case flung out, snowy peaks and clouds behind; at the end it raises both arms high. (1s)
