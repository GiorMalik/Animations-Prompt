# O88 Spotlight

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Indie Era 2010–2019
- What it looks like: A picture printed in newspaper halftone, set on the grey-green screen of a microfiche reader: scanlines, a rolling scan band of light, dust on the glass. The film's reporters dig the truth out of exactly this kind of archive, and the shot changes are made as the film slides sideways in the reader.
- Style: Microfiche reader · newspaper halftone
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2015

## Style Prompt

A 2001 newspaper colour halftone on a library microfiche reader. Signature shot: the picture fills the frame, no headline or columns; lines and lettering inside it stay solid. CMYK screens at 15°, 75°, 0°, 45°, dots about 1/250 of frame width, 1 px misregistration. Convert to low-contrast phosphor-green monochrome, dots still clearly visible: film base #1b2320, frame gaps #0c110f, highlights no brighter than #a0dcaf. Only the story's one hero keeps its colour, whole figure (e.g. #D97757), screened and equally low-contrast; every light source turns green. Scan lines every 3 px, a faint scan bar every 4 s, a lamp hot-spot, dust, a hair, a light rounded hood vignette (outer ~5%), a small monospace fiche index. Scene changes slide the fiche sideways past a dark gap (0.5 s); slow push-ins. Avoid: newspaper layout, colour leaking, hard cuts.

## Supplement

### Director's notes

Clawd plays a Spotlight reporter: a lanyard ID badge, a shorthand notebook, later swapped for a highlighter. Three microfiche frames, joined not by hard cuts but by the film sliding sideways in the reader, the last beat landing on the front page.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Wide of the basement newsroom: reporters around a table piled with paper, a fluorescent-light grid overhead, Clawd standing at the front with its badge and shorthand notebook; the camera slowly pulling back. (0.8s)
2. The film sliding sideways to the next frame: a top-down shot of the printed priest list, a toy-sized Clawd pushing a big highlighter down the rows line by line, the camera moving down with it. (0.8s)
3. Sliding again to the press dock: a medium close-up, Clawd standing beside the front page, its headline 'Church allowed abuse by priest for years'; a slow push. (0.9s)
