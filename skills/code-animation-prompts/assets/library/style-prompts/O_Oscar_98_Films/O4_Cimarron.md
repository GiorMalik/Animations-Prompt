# O4 Cimarron

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Silent & Early Sound 1927–1938
- What it looks like: An 1889 gold-toned albumen-print panorama: soft focus, slight horizontal drag, cream highlights and warm brown shadows, silver grain, with only Clawd hand-coloured. The film opens on the 1889 Oklahoma land rush, so it is shot in the photographic texture of that year — and this chapter stays monochrome.
- Style: 1889 sepia albumen-print panorama
- Keywords: 2D·Sepia
- Year: 1930/31

## Style Prompt

A moving 1880s gold-toned albumen photograph. Paint soft-edged flat shapes by brightness: main subjects dark (15-30%), ground 50-60%, sky about 90%; visible hatching and grass tufts (lines ~1/300 of frame width). Then print: grayscale, slight blur (~1/800 of frame width), a faint smear of three horizontal offset copies growing with camera speed, multiply cream #F2DCB4, lift blacks to umber #2E1C0F, blotchy development (±5%), vignette to #785634, per-frame grain (±4%), dust, scratches. Hand-tint only the hero the story centres on (e.g. #D97757) as transparent dye, mask feathered 2-3 px; lamps stay sepia. Wide panorama: the middle band packed with repeated small elements (figures, animals, vehicles; for a lone subject fences, posts, trees), no big plane dominating. Camera: one 4-5 s ease-out pull back from the hero to the panorama. Avoid: hard ink outlines, colour beyond the hero, a flat grey print.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Yancey Cravat: white wide-brimmed cowboy hat, neckerchief, riding a black horse at the front. The whole passage runs at 1.1×. Pull back first to see more horses, then one cut back to a close-up; on beat 5 it draws its gun and fires into the sky.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Starting on a close-up of Clawd riding the black horse at the front, pulling all the way out to a wide: covered wagons, stagecoaches, a mass of riders in the dust. (1.5s)
2. Cut to a close-up of Clawd on horseback: it raises its revolver and fires into the sky, the frame jolting with the recoil; a gentle push. (1s)
