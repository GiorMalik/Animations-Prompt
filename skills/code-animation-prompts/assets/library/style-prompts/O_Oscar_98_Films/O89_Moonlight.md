# O89 Moonlight

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Indie Era 2010–2019
- What it looks like: Full teal, magenta and moonlight-blue oil portraits, sculpted heads repainted with strokes that follow the forms. Painted straight from the film's line — 'In moonlight, black boys look blue.'
- Style: Moonlight-blue oil portrait
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2016

## Style Prompt

Night oil portraiture: smooth volumes repainted in two passes of short, low-contrast (±8% value) form-following strokes: broad (1/60 of frame width) on water, sky and cloth, fine (1/150) on faces, which stay smooth. Palette by role: sky, sea and far land mid blue-violet #32377a, about 60% of the frame; moonlit planes and sand pale lavender-blue; teal #128a91 in water and lit sides; magenta #7a2f86 rim; deepest shadows #07071c. Any skin takes blue top planes and a wet highlight, eyes open and lit. Default night: one key, a full moon with a broken glitter path on water; magenta only as a soft rim from a distant glow; other small light sources stay cyan-white points. Grain ±3%. Signature shot: the figure calm, ~2/3 of frame height. Slow push-ins. Reference: Barry Jenkins, *Moonlight* (2016). Avoid: coarse dabs, small dark statue eyes, a murky frame.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Oil portrait brushwork, teal / magenta / blue-violet duotone, a camera half above and half below the water

### Director's notes

Clawd plays little Chiron, no costume: first held up in the water at a swimming lesson, then standing between the three faces, finally sitting on the beach beside Kevin. Three chapters, three hard-cut shots; both the sea and the beach shots push in slowly, letting the quiet carry on.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A half-above, half-below shot at the teal sea surface: a man standing chest-deep in the water, holding Clawd up with his palm, his hand visible below the waterline; a slow push. (0.8s)
2. Cut to three oil portraits side by side, teal, magenta and blue-violet one each; the camera reframing as the three faces slide together into a wedge-shaped collage, Clawd standing in the teal panel. (0.8s)
3. Cut to the moonlit beach: Kevin and Clawd sitting side by side in a two-shot on the blue sand, a full moon at the upper right, waves breaking on the shore; a slow push. (0.9s)
