- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Fin de Siècle 1990–1999

## Style Prompt

An illuminated manuscript page (cropped, or an open spread) filling the frame. Vellum #ECDCB4, brown ink outlines #2A1A10, lapis #27469A, vermilion #C43A20, burnished gold leaf on raised gesso, with punched dot rosettes; a slow candle glint sweeps the gold only. Miniature backgrounds are mostly gold (night: lapis with gold stars); gold fills at least a tenth of the frame. Gothic figures in flat colour and ink outline. Celtic interlace, ivy borders, marginal grotesques; the story's text (Latin or the user's language) in broad-nib textura, red-and-blue line fillers, one large gilded initial. Layout: the miniature a window about half the page beside the text column, layered for parallax on pushes; figures may break out. Warm candle from upper left, brown vignette, pigment grain. References: Book of Kells, Morgan Bible, Luttrell Psalter. Avoid: flat yellow for gold; modern typefaces; glints on faces.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Wallace: a two-handed sword, a tartan plaid, a stripe of blue woad across its forehead. Four hard cuts: the initial, the whole page, the bridge, the clash. The charge grows fiercer toward the middle, and in the last shot a scroll unfurls overhead reading freedom. Scene time is about 1.8× screen time.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Close-up of the gilded initial F, with Wallace's face half-painted in blue woad inside the letter's counter, a shaft of gold light sweeping across the leaf. (0.7s)
2. A hard cut to the whole page: REEDOM, the Gothic text, the margins, with the Stirling Bridge miniature in the middle and Clawd charging at the front; a gentle push. (0.6s)
3. A hard cut into the miniature: English soldiers squeezing across the wooden bridge, a knight in a checkered horse-cloth charging in, men struggling in the river. (0.6s)
4. A hard cut to a close-up of Clawd: it raises its two-handed sword against the knight's lance, and a speech scroll unfurls overhead reading freedom. (0.6s)
