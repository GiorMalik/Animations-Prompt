# O71 Shakespeare in Love

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Fin de Siècle 1990–1999
- What it looks like: An Elizabethan court miniature after Nicholas Hilliard: opaque watercolour on vellum, an ultramarine ground, shell-gold lettering, white wild roses. The film is set in Shakespeare's London, and the miniature is the portrait form of that very court.
- Style: Hilliard Elizabethan miniature
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1998

## Style Prompt

Elizabethan portrait miniature in Nicholas Hilliard's manner: opaque watercolour laid flat on warm vellum, very fine hatching in shadows (lines about 1/1000 of frame width), lead-white dots on lit edges, red-brown outlines. Palette: ultramarine ground #203E92, shell gold #D6B05C (granular flecks, a few burnished highlights), carmine #A8202C, lead white #F6F2E8, verdigris #346C40. Signature shot: the picture inside a gold-rimmed oval locket lying on rust-red damask; a flowering white eglantine briar fills the oval behind and around the subject; a short motto in gold Roman capitals arcs along the top. The oval's ground stays ultramarine; landscape and sky go in as flat colour bands. Wide scenes may leave the locket, same method, true perspective. Slow push, slight tilt; only flowers, cloth and gestures move. Reference: Hilliard, *Young Man Among Roses* (c. 1587). Avoid: airbrush gradients; thick oil strokes; flat yellow gold.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Court miniature portraiture (limning), opaque watercolour on vellum, shell gold, dark red damask still life

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Shakespeare: a starched ruff, a gold earring, a quill in the miniature, a white rose on stage. Four hard-cut shots move from the private miniature to the public theatre, and on the last beat the Queen slowly nods, closing the sequence.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Close-up: the gold pendant miniature on dark red damask, Clawd as Shakespeare among white wild roses, wearing a ruff, its quill writing; the camera slowly pushes in and tilts down slightly. (0.8s)
2. Wide: the Rose Theatre. The camera tilts down from the painted sky and the stands and pushes in to the stage, where Clawd stands holding a white rose and the Queen sits in the box at the right. (0.6s)
3. Medium shot: the balcony scene, Juliet above resting her chin on her hand, Clawd below holding the white rose up to her; a slight push. (0.6s)
4. Close-up: Queen Elizabeth in her canopied box, turning from the play, fanning herself, nodding slowly; a slight push. (0.6s)
