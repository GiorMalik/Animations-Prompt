# O45 The Godfather

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · New Hollywood 1970–1979
- What it looks like: An oil painting in deep amber and ochre-brown, warm only where the top light falls: the shadows are transparent thin glazes, the lit edges impasto. Recreating Gordon Willis's top light in the 'I believe in America' study.
- Style: Rembrandt top-light chiaroscuro oil painting
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1972

## Style Prompt

Chiaroscuro oil painting after Rembrandt and Caravaggio, one overhead light. About 70% of the frame is readable warm dark, mostly umber #24150B, near-black #070403 only in the deepest corners. Amber #8F5D2A mid-tones about 20%; top planes reach #E2B673. The hero stands in the light pool, lit area at least 1/4 of frame height; brows shade the eyes. One red accent #9C1A1A. Darks thin smooth glazes; lights thick impasto, ridged strokes about 1/100 of frame width; modelling soft, never scratchy. Amber varnish, heavy vignette, canvas grain (±3%). A warm bright opening (window, doorway, horizon) may glow behind; a small lamp in frame is a small glow, not a second key. Camera: slow pull-backs and pushes. Reference: Francis Ford Coppola, *The Godfather* (1972), cinematographer Gordon Willis. Avoid: lifting the darks, flat light, faces popping out, frames too dark to read.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Chiaroscuro oil painting, Gordon Willis's top light, Rembrandt- and Caravaggio-style glazing

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Don Corleone: black bow tie, wing collar, red rose, white cuffs, one hand resting on the cat's back. All 12 beats run at 1×, keeping Willis's patient camera; stroking the cat lasts about 4 seconds, with one blink in the middle. All hard cuts; in the last shot the puppet-master hand from the original poster descends onto its head.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Starting on Bonasera's grey hair in the dark, the camera slowly pulls back to reveal the Godfather seated in the wingback chair with the cat before his knees. (1.7s)
2. Close-up: Clawd under the top light slowly strokes the grey tabby, bow tie and red rose catching the light; a slow push. (1s)
3. Pushing toward the blinds: the wedding outside in sunlight, soft and overexposed, with a black-robed silhouette at the left. (0.8s)
4. Wide of the study, the camera pushing slightly: the puppeteer's hand descends from the top of the frame, the strings slowly tightening on Clawd. (1.5s)
