# O75 Chicago

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · New Millennium 2000–2009
- What it looks like: A black stage cut open by hard-edged spotlights: silhouettes of the women prisoners edged in red in six cells, a neon C behind, sequins and smoke, Clawd in a white follow spot. This is the film's 'Cell Block Tango', in the poster's red and black.
- Style: Fosse stage: red-and-black spotlight silhouettes
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2002

## Style Prompt

Bob Fosse-style Broadway stage number. Black stage, but about 40% of the frame glows signal red #D0202A: a row of dark barred cells, each backlit red through haze, bright at centre, falling off to black, plus red curtains; hard-edged spots. Dancers are full-bodied black silhouettes with costume shapes and a 2–3 px red rim. One big neon shape (letter or pictogram) in orange-red #FF5A3C, white-hot core, slight flicker, set against black so it reads; glossy black floor reflects it; marquee bulbs #FFD9A0 at the stage edge. The hero, in a warm-white follow spot #FFF1D6, is the only lit figure in its own colours. Accents: sequins, white gloves. Moves on the jazz beat; cells snap on one by one. Push in to end in the follow spot. Reference: Rob Marshall, *Chicago* (2002). Avoid: flat even fills; extra colours; rimless silhouettes.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Bob Fosse's stage staging, red-edged backlit silhouettes, neon and sequins, the 2002 poster's red and black

### Director's notes

Clawd wears a Fosse-style bowler (a ring of sequins around the band) and white gloves, holding a black cane. In the first shot the cells light up one by one on the beat; a fast cut to one prisoner; then Clawd takes the follow spot alone, finishing with jazz hands. The performance runs at real speed, not accelerated.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Wide of the stage, the neon C behind: the six cells light up one by one, each woman prisoner snapping into her signature pose, Clawd walking Fosse-style at centre stage; a slow push. (0.8s)
2. Close-up: one cell, Velma's silhouette in the red light behind the bars, swivelling her hips and shrugging; a slight push. No Clawd. (0.5s)
3. Medium close-up: Clawd alone in a white follow spot, the lit cells behind it: it walks Fosse-style to the footlights, then puts the cane down and raises both hands in jazz hands; a slow push. (1.2s)
