# O44 The French Connection

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · New Hollywood 1970–1979
- What it looks like: A whole Brooklyn street beneath the elevated railway built in 3D, then graded to coarse 16mm grain: cold grey, shadows leaning blue, high contrast, gate weave. The film is most famous for its car chase under the el — only 3D can shoot that kind of speed.
- Style: Chase under the el · 16mm grain 3D
- Keywords: 3D
- Year: 1971

## Style Prompt

Gritty early-1970s documentary look in a detailed 3D world, finished as pushed 16mm. Grade: low saturation, high contrast; neutral-cold greys #7A7C7A, cyan shadows #1E2A2C, warm-brown mid-tones #6E4A34 in brick, wood, vehicles (at least a quarter of the frame), never an overall blue; the one hero (e.g. #D97757) stays saturated. Default overcast, sky near-white, at any time of day; sun through any overhead grid (track, bridge, pier) falls in hard stripes. Grain clumps about 1/700 of frame width, ±8% brightness; gate weave, flicker, dust, occasional faint ghosts of bright lights. Dense clutter fitting the place. Handheld cameras ride the action: a low 18–25 mm lens rushing forward as repeated objects strobe past, a rig looking back at the subject, a corner wide. References: William Friedkin, *The French Connection* (1971); cinematographer Owen Roizman. Avoid: clean CG shine, blue tint, empty places, mushy grain.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays 'Popeye' Doyle: a porkpie hat, angry eyes, both hands on the wheel. The chase's scene time runs much faster than screen time, so the acceleration just reads as the car going faster. Three hard cuts: intersection fishtail → bumper rush → driver, without stopping in between.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Wide of the intersection: a train passes overhead, the brown Pontiac fishtails onto the street, smoke from its rear wheels; the camera's viewpoint slowly widens. (0.8s)
2. The film's famous bumper camera: an ultra-low camera rushing forward with motion blur, the el's pillars flashing past one by one. (0.8s)
3. Medium shot, seen from outside through the lowered window, of Clawd as Doyle: porkpie hat, gritting its teeth on the steering wheel, the el and shopfronts sliding past behind, with in-car shake. (0.9s)
