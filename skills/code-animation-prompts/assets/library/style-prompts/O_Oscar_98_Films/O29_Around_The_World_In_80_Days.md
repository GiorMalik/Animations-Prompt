# O29 Around the World in 80 Days

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Widescreen 1950–1959
- What it looks like: 1950s flat geometric cartoon: colour blocks and ink lines misregistered in overprint, broken ink lines, crayon grain on cream paper. This film's 6-minute end-title animation is by Saul Bass — exactly this modernist drawing style.
- Style: 1950s UPA modernist cartoon
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1956

## Style Prompt

1950s UPA modernist cartoon. Uneven ink lines #221C19 about 1/400 of frame width, sometimes broken; colour blocks sit about 1/300 of frame width off them, all one way. Objects are overlapping flat cut-out facets: a lit face and a shadow face one flat step (about 15%) darker. Pale aqua sky and cream paper #F2E9D3 cover most of the frame; mustard #E3A92B, teal #2B8C86, orange-red #D4452B are accents; sky, greens and slate blues are these mixed toward paper. Other times of day change only the sky colour. Crayon grain ±5% brightness everywhere, few flecks. Poster layout: one big graphic shape (a building or sun disc), angular outlined figures beside it. Light beams: flat pale wedges. Hard cuts every 1–2 s; slow parallax drift. After UPA, *Gerald McBoing-Boing* (1950); Saul Bass, *Around the World in 80 Days* titles (1956). Avoid: gradients, perfect registration, saturated colour everywhere.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

UPA limited animation, Saul Bass end-title animation, 1956 original poster palette, crayon-grain print

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Fogg: a top hat, a furled umbrella, a glass of champagne. One hard cut every two beats: map, wide, close-up. The red route runs once around the globe, the balloon drifts over the Alps, and the close-up lands on Passepartout scooping snow off a peak to chill the champagne.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Map insert, locked-off camera: a globe turning on a teal ground, the red route running through London, Suez, Bombay, Yokohama, San Francisco in turn; a little balloon at the right with Clawd riding in it. (0.9s)
2. Wide: a mustard-yellow balloon with a blue chequered band drifting over faceted Alps and a Swiss town, Clawd in its top hat standing at the edge of the basket; a slow lateral move. (0.8s)
3. Close-up of Clawd as Fogg: top hat, furled umbrella, champagne glass raised, Passepartout leaning out beside it to scoop snow from a snowy peak with an ice bucket; a slight push in. (0.8s)
