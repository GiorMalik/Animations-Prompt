# O28 Marty

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Widescreen 1950–1959
- What it looks like: A 1955 television recording, playing on a TV in a warm-coloured Bronx living room: scanlines, phosphor glow, a rounded bulging screen, snow — only Clawd in colour. The original was a live TV drama, so it is shot as television.
- Style: 1950s TV kinescope recording
- Keywords: 2D (B&W screen)
- Year: 1955

## Style Prompt

A 1950s kinescope playing on a wood-cabinet television. Inside the screen: black-and-white with a cool phosphor tint (#DCE6EA whites), soft (half resolution, scaled up), halation, ghosting, tearing bands, a rolling hum bar, light snow, lifted blacks, flicker, grain; only the story's hero keeps its colour (e.g. #D97757), under the same artefacts. Tube: rounded corners, sides bowing outward, about 330 scanlines, glass reflections. Outside, a warm flat-illustrated living room (ochre wallpaper, wood, a table lamp), lit warm by the lamp; the screen casts a clear cold-blue glow on nearby walls. Signature shot: hold about 2 s with the TV filling 60% of the frame, then push through the glass (4-5 s) until the broadcast fills the frame, scanlines visible, then steady TV-studio mid shots. Reference: director Delbert Mann's live Philco Television Playhouse dramas. Avoid: heavy snow, a dull, evenly lit room.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Kinescope B&W recording, rounded CRT and scanlines, warm 1950s living-room illustration

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Marty: a white butcher-paper hat and a striped tie, shyly leaning against a pillar. Pushing through the glass takes us from the living room into the show; in the two-shot it gathers its courage and crosses the dance floor; the last hard cut lands on the invitation to dance — it holds out its hand, and Clara's smile comes a moment later.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Mum's living room: a table lamp and a rotary phone on the left, her wingback chair on the right, the Stardust Ballroom playing on the TV; the camera pushes through the screen glass. (0.8s)
2. A two-shot across the dance floor, scanlines still visible: Clawd in its butcher-paper hat and striped tie walks from the pillar toward Clara; the camera tracks right. (0.9s)
3. Mid-shot of the invitation: Clawd holds out its hand, Clara looks up at it and smiles; the camera pushes gently and drifts right. (0.8s)
