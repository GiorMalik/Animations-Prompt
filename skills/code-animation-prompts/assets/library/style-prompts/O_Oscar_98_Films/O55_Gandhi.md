# O55 Gandhi

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Blockbuster Era 1980–1989
- What it looks like: A hand block print on cotton: indigo, madder red, turmeric yellow plus black carved outlines, misregistration at the block seams, the dye bleeding along the weave. Handspun khadi is Gandhi's symbol, so the Salt March is printed as a piece of cloth.
- Style: Rajasthani hand block print cloth
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1982

## Style Prompt

Hand block print on cotton, Rajasthan style (Bagru, Sanganer). Undyed cotton #EEE5CF is the ground, over half the frame, and the skin colour; indigo #2A3A68 for water or sky bands and patterns; madder red #AA322A for borders, clothing and the hero; turmeric #DEA42C for small motifs; black key block #281E1C for outlines. Stamp blocks about 1/8 of frame height, misregistered 2–4 px, tone jumping slightly at seams even in large bands; dye bleeds along the weave, fills unevenly. Mud-resist crackle only inside indigo, fine and sparse. Carved, rough outlines about 1/300 of frame width. Flat frieze in a paisley and vine border; waves as scallop rows; profile figures with miniature-painting faces, kohl-lined eyes. Camera: pan along the frieze, push in; cut on a turning motif. Avoid: vector edges, perfect registration, faceless figures, dense crackle, indigo flooding the frame.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Bagru mud-resist indigo, Sanganer block-print borders, Rajasthani miniature profile faces

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Gandhi: round steel-rimmed glasses, the edge of a khadi shawl, a bamboo staff, a handful of salt. The whole thing at 1.1×, no slow motion. A hard cut from the march to the action: bend down, scoop the salt, raise it, then turn to the march and raise it higher. The ending uses the spinning wheel as a wipe into the next film.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Panning along the seaside march: volunteers in white khadi, women in indigo and madder-red saris, the self-rule flag; Clawd enters at the head of the march. (0.8s)
2. Medium close-up, the camera pushing in slowly: Clawd in round glasses and khadi shawl bends down to scoop a handful of salt, raises it so the grains sparkle, then turns to the march and raises it higher. (1.1s)
3. A village woman spinning at her wheel; the camera pushes toward the turning wheel, cutting into the next film. (0.6s)
