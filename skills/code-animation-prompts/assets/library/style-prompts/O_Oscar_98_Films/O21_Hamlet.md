# O21 Hamlet

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Colour & War 1939–1949
- What it looks like: Elsinore in real-time 3D, high-contrast deep-focus black and white: volumetric fog, wet stone, waves breaking into lace foam, silver grain. Shot after the castle, the battlements in the fog and the rocks in Olivier's film; Clawd is the only colour.
- Style: B&W 3D castle in the fog
- Keywords: 3D·B&W
- Year: 1948

## Style Prompt

A real 3D scene in late-1940s deep-focus black-and-white. Built forms: bare masonry, block by block, bevelled, staggered (Romanesque arches only where the setting has them); wet surfaces dark and glossy. Soft overcast light, no hard shadows: dark masses against a silver sky. Low fog fills the lower third; the far background ends at 60-70% grey, the midground sharp; a bright patch behind cloud throws faint shafts. Water, if any: layered swells, lace foam, misty spray. Post: ambient occlusion, gentle bloom, high-contrast silver grade (true blacks, unclipped whites), grain (±3%), faint flicker. Only the hero the story centres on keeps its colour (e.g. #D97757); every light source stays grey. Camera: slow drift in, a track along a high edge, a low angle on the hero. Reference: Laurence Olivier, *Hamlet* (1948). Avoid: hard sunlit shadows, white-out fog, tiled textures.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

three.js 3D + volumetric fog, Desmond Dickinson deep-focus B&W, Roger Furse's bare Romanesque sets

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Hamlet: Yorick's skull, and the white lace cuffs, belt and dagger from the poster. Three hard cuts walk through the film's three images: the castle on the cliff, the ghost in the fog, the rock of the soliloquy; in the last shot the skull is raised on the beat. The performance runs at 1.8×.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A wide seen from the sea: Elsinore on the headland, waves breaking at its foot, Clawd a small figure on the reef top at the right; the camera drifts closer slowly. (0.7s)
2. A wall-walk in thick fog: the armoured ghost takes shape out of the fog and beckons, Clawd watching it from nearby; a gentle push. (0.8s)
3. A low camera looking up at the seaside rock: Clawd in lace cuffs with a dagger at its belt raises Yorick's skull before the castle in the fog; a slow push. (1.1s)
