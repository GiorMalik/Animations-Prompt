# O33 The Apartment

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Swinging Sixties 1960–1969
- What it looks like: Saul Bass-style white lines sliding in on black, building the film's most famous one-point-perspective endless office hall, then filled with grey tones. The film's titles were Bass's work; the only warm colour in the whole frame is Clawd and the key tag.
- Style: Saul Bass line title sequence · B&W
- Keywords: 2D·B&W
- Year: 1960

## Style Prompt

Saul Bass title lines turned into a set. A shot opens on black: thin white lines (about 1/800 of frame width) slide in and grow into a grid; by 2–3 s grey planes fill in and lights switch on row by row. Then hold the finished frame 3 s: mid-to-light grey, flat fills in 4–5 even steps from #140F0D to #F4F1E8, every shape outlined in dark line about 1/600 of frame width. Strict one-point perspective, centred vanishing point; identical elements repeat into depth (desks and people, or posts, lamps, windows). Figures: flat two-tone shapes. The only warm colour is orange-red #E2472A, for the one hero subject and one prop; all light stays white. Camera: a fast push to the hero, a hard cut to top-down, the grid becoming pattern. Avoid: sketchy lines, gradients, colour leaks, skewed perspective, unfilled wireframe.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Saul Bass title design, one-point-perspective office hall, B&W film grain, the only warm colour

### Director's notes

Clawd plays C. C. Baxter: a bowler hat, the apartment key hanging on an orange-red tag (51 W 67), sitting on the edge of a front-row desk. The office hall is drawn line by line; on beat 2 it raises the key and the camera pushes in fast; the last cut is a top-down view of the grid of desks, a graphic match with the next film's opening aerial of the Manhattan street grid.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. One continuous take: white lines build the endless office hall out of total black, grey tones and rows of ceiling lights switching on; Clawd as Baxter, sitting on the edge of a front-row desk, raises the apartment key on beat 2 as the camera pushes in fast. (1.9s)
2. A hard cut to a straight top-down view: the desks form a grid, Clawd lying face-up on its own desk with the key and tag in hand; a slow push in. (0.6s)
