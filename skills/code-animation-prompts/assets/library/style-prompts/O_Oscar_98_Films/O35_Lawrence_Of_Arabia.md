# O35 Lawrence of Arabia

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Swinging Sixties 1960–1969
- What it looks like: Epic poster gouache with visible brush direction, strong warm-cold contrast: amber desert against cobalt-blue sky. Lawrence of Arabia's original poster was painted exactly this way by Howard Terpning.
- Style: Terpning epic poster gouache
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1962

## Style Prompt

1960s hand-painted epic film poster, gouache with oil highlights. Draw forms solidly, then paint: an opaque mid-tone base, directional strokes following each form (1/80–1/50 of frame width on large areas, smaller on faces and hands), soft-blended edges; model every form in three values; dry-brush only on dust, cloth and distant figures; sharp warm-cream highlights last on lit edges. Warm–cool contrast: cobalt sky #1F4D97 to pale blue #A9C3D6, warming to cream near a low sun; ground #EEC27A (any terrain); ochre-red #B56A33 for skin, earth and costume; violet #6C6394 for distant shadows. The hero centred and largest, a diagonal gesture, cloth streaming; smaller, looser figures may recede behind. Light board grain (±3%), warm vignette, haze. Motion: bounce, slow push to a low close-up, a whip pan out. Avoid: scratchy uniform hatching, flat digital fills, cold-only colour.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Gouache plus oil poster painting, Howard Terpning 1962 poster, Freddie Young 70mm desert light

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Lawrence: white robe, white headcloth, gold headband, riding a camel and raising a curved sword. The opening is cinema's most famous match cut: the last film's struck match becomes the desert sunrise. The whole charge runs at a constant 1.5×, the sword raised on the beat with a flash of its tip, then a whip-pan out of frame into Tom Jones.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A vast desert dawn in extreme wide: a huge sun rising dead centre through the heat haze, a match cut with the match blown out in the last film; a slow push in. (0.7s)
2. A poster composition on a tracking vehicle: Clawd as Lawrence riding its camel in among the charging horsemen, sword held low at first, then raised on the beat with a flash of its tip; bumpy shake, a slow push in. (1s)
3. A low angle pushing toward the raised sword, then a whip-pan right out of the frame into the next film. (0.8s)
