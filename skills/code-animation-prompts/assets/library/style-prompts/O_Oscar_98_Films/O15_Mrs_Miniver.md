# O15 Mrs. Miniver

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Colour & War 1939–1949
- What it looks like: A WWII British home-front lithograph poster: a limited overprint of brick red, deep green, cream and grey-blue, with shadows in lithographic crayon grain. Set to the film's ending: the whole village singing in a village church whose roof has been bombed open, Spitfires flying over the hole.
- Style: British wartime lithograph poster
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1942

## Style Prompt

A 1942 British wartime stone-lithograph poster. Simple, strong shapes from three plates, 1-3 px off register: a colour plate with all flat fills; a grey-blue crayon plate darkening every shadow with short slanted stone grain; a green-black key line about 1/500 of frame width around every shape, figures and clouds included. Palette by role: cream #EBDFC2 paper and highlights, grey-blue #8497A6 sky and shadow, brick red #A23F2D and deep green #34503F as accents, at most a quarter of the frame; browns and skin mixed from cream and brick; key #1E2422. Scenes may be full. Airbrushed skies; only the paper grain boils at 12 fps. Pale light bands, angled from the source, cancel the shadow plate. Figures stand frontally. Camera: slow drifts, a tilt up. References: Frank Newbould, Abram Games. Avoid: dot screens, clean vector edges, big saturated fields.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Mrs Miniver: a lace-trimmed straw hat, a hymn book, and a red 'Mrs Miniver' rose. In the wide it sings with the congregation; cutting to the close-up it hears the engines, stops and looks up; the third shot is its point of view, through the hole in the roof, watching the Spitfire formation fly over. Three hard cuts, joined by one upward look.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Wide, looking down the nave of the bombed church: villagers singing between the pews, sunlight falling in columns; Clawd in a lace-trimmed straw hat sits in the front row, singing with a hymn book and a red rose in hand. The camera pulls back slightly. (0.8s)
2. Close-up of Clawd and the rose: the sound of engines arrives, it stops singing and looks up toward the roof. A gentle push. (0.6s)
3. Looking straight up from inside the church at the hole in the roof: broken rafters framing the sky, Spitfires flying past in a V formation. The camera pushes in slowly and rotates slightly. (1.1s)
