# O65 Unforgiven

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Fin de Siècle 1990–1999
- What it looks like: A thickly painted Western oil in two registers: Remington's golden-red sunset plains, and his later blue-green night scenes lit by warm yellow window light. Unforgiven opens and closes with a silhouette before a grave at sunset, and the showdown takes place in a rainy town at night.
- Style: Remington Western oil painting · sunset and rainy night
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1992

## Style Prompt

Frederic Remington-style Western oil painting. Primary register, for anything before full dark: sunset, a red-gold sky (orange into deep red) in long horizontal strokes, the sun on the horizon, land and figures dark warm silhouettes, thin warm rim sunward. Second register, only after dark: a blue-green nocturne (sky #17333A, moonlit edges #9CC4BC, warm window light #FFCC6A on dark ground #1E1C16, rain). Broad, readable shapes first, then loaded strokes about 1/80 of frame width wide, 3–6 times as long, soft broken ends, each within ±6% brightness of the colour beneath, following the surface. Light canvas texture. Low horizon, sky 50–60% of the frame, figures small to medium. Camera: one slow push from a silhouetted wide to the figure. References: Remington's late nocturnes; cinematographer Jack N. Green (*Unforgiven*, 1992). Avoid: smooth gradients; hard-edged flat bands; shattered shapes; grey figures in warm scenes; wide pans.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Remington's warm-coloured plains, Remington's late nocturnes, thick oil paint, the opening titles' silhouettes

### Director's notes

Clawd plays William Munny: a black wide-brimmed hat, a long raincoat with its collar up, a rifle. The opening silhouette to the grave is one shot, and as the push lands it is just looking up; the only hard cut goes to the rainy night, where Clawd rides into town. 1:1 time, no slowing.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. One push from the whole sunset (a cabin, a windmill, a lone tree, a cross silhouetted against the sun) to Clawd as Munny beside the grave; it raises its head and holds the rifle across its body. (1.4s)
2. A hard cut to the rainy night in Big Whiskey: Clawd rides a rabbit-brown horse down the mud street, Greeley's saloon with its lit windows beside it, the camera pushing toward its dripping hat brim. (1.1s)
