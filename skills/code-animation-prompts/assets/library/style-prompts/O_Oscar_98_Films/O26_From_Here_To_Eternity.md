# O26 From Here to Eternity

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Widescreen 1950–1959
- What it looks like: 1953 high-key black and white: hard sunlight, darkened skies, whitened foam, wet sand reflecting the sky, silver grain and halation. Shot for the kiss in the surf at Halona Cove; Clawd sitting on the lava blowing the bugle is the only colour.
- Style: High-key beach B&W screen
- Keywords: 2D·B&W
- Year: 1953

## Style Prompt

Early-1950s high-key black-and-white photography, painted in 2D to read as a photo: no outlines or flat fills, only soft gradients plus fine texture (sand, rock pitting, cloth). Values: about 70% of the frame above mid-grey; sky light-mid grey, one step below the bright clouds; deep blacks small. Default: hard midday sun, short crisp shadows; under other light keep the pale sky and bright values. Water, foam and mist blow out at the peaks, keeping lace detail; wet surfaces mirror the sky. Print finish: gentle S-curve, slightly soft lens, halation, fresh fine grain (±4%), light vignette, flicker. Only the hero the story centres on keeps colour, whole figure (e.g. #D97757), shaded photographically. Camera: tilt down from the sky, tight level frame, slow pull-back. Reference: cinematographer Burnett Guffey (*From Here to Eternity*). Avoid: low-key frames, white blanks, cartoon fills.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

1953 high-key B&W photography, Burnett Guffey, silver grain and halation

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Prewitt: an olive drab garrison cap, holding the bugle from the poster. The sky tilts down into the bay in one shot; in the middle shot the waves wash over the kiss; cutting to the wide, Clawd raises the bugle to its lips on the beat, as if sounding the call for the scene.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Opening above Halona Cove, a squadron of planes flying through the piled clouds; the camera tilts down to the sea. (0.5s)
2. Eye-level, tightly framed on the lovers in the surf: a wave rushes up and covers them, then pulls back into lace-like foam, the two kissing all the while; the camera pushes in slowly and drifts right. (1.1s)
3. Wide of the bay: Clawd in its garrison cap sitting on a lava rock platform, raising the bugle toward the lovers and sounding it; the camera pulls back slightly. (0.9s)
