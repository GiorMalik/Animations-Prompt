# O10 The Life of Emile Zola

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Silent & Early Sound 1927–1938
- What it looks like: Newspaper letterpress plus 1890s line engraving: big wooden-type headlines, densely set body type, ink spread and drop-out on yellowing newsprint, figures drawn in hatching. The film's most famous image is the front page of L'Aurore for 13 January 1898, 'J'Accuse…!'.
- Style: Newspaper letterpress + line engraving
- Keywords: 2D·B&W
- Year: 1937

## Style Prompt

1890s newspaper print. Set text as a sharp mask, then print: ~1 px blur and a noisy threshold for ink spread, ragged edges, under-inked patches, faint mirrored show-through. Page grammar: masthead, a wood-type headline across the width (~1/6 of frame height), 3-6 columns of small type in the user's language (~1/80 of frame height) flanking one large engraved picture. Wood-type letters are grained blocks. Everything pictured is a line engraving: hatching lines about 1/1000 of frame width, spaced 1/400, following the form, cross-hatching only in darks, heavier shadow-side contours, paler distance. Warm black #17130F on newsprint #E8E0CC; the hero the story centres on gets one accent plate (e.g. #D97757), offset 1-2 px. Motion: type blocks slam down, bounce and leave their print; one repeating mechanical motion, pages turning. Avoid: clean digital type, smooth grey shading, other colours.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Wooden-type headlines, newsprint letterpress, 1890s line engraving

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Zola: pince-nez, quill pen, holding the day's newspaper. On beat 2 the last letter drops and the whole page jolts; on beat 3 it looks up from the newspaper, quill held high. The scene runs at 1.05×, two hard cuts: headline → Zola → the press.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Close-up of the L'Aurore front page (JEUDI 13 JANVIER 1898): the wooden type of 'J'Accuse…!' slams down letter by letter, and the whole page jolts as the last one lands; the camera pushes in. (0.9s)
2. Mid-shot: Clawd as Zola standing before the front page, looking up from the newspaper in its hands, jumping and raising its quill high, a newsboy hawking on the right; a gentle pull. (0.8s)
3. The rotary press turning, a pressman lifting a wet newspaper, papers flying off one by one; the camera pulls back and moves right. (0.8s)
