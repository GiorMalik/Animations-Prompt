# O78 Crash

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · New Millennium 2000–2009
- What it looks like: A finely rendered night photograph drawn in code: white and red car-light trails on the interchange, sodium orange against neon cyan, wet-road reflections, bokeh, and snow visible only in the light. Crash ends on a rare Los Angeles snowfall.
- Style: LA night light trails + falling snow
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2005

## Style Prompt

Long-exposure night street photography. Sodium-vapour orange #F2A24A washes structures, signs and wet ground (about half the frame in warm light), against cyan neon #3FD0D8 and the odd red-blue emergency light; blacks cyan-tinged, never grey. Layered composition: foreground pavement with figures, lit shopfronts and signs mid-frame, roads or bridges crossing the upper third (away from cities, a lamp-lit road in the subject's own setting). Headlights become thin continuous ribbons (about 1/150 of frame width) along those roads, white #FFF4E0 and red #E0303A, glowing with hot cores. Snow: three layers of short soft streaks, seen only in light cones. Finish: two-level bloom, blue lens streaks, edge bokeh, vignette, moving grain. The hero stands sharp in a lamp cone mid-frame; only traffic streaks. Camera: one continuous tilt, pan and rack focus. Reference: cinematographer J. Michael Muro (*Crash*, 2004). Avoid: evenly lit snow; empty black frames; dotted trails.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Long-exposure night photography, sodium orange with neon cyan, anamorphic light streaks and film grain

### Director's notes

Clawd wears a hand-knitted dark cyan scarf and stands under a streetlight looking up at the snow. One continuous camera move from the light trails to the crash to Clawd, the fast-moving stretches carrying motion blur. By the time the camera reaches it the snow is falling harder, and the gesture of catching a flake lands near the end.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. One continuous take: opening looking up at the light trails on the interchange, tilting down to the rear-end crash behind the cordon, focus on the accident; then panning right along the kerb to Clawd under the sodium light, focus pulling over; Clawd raises an arm to catch a snowflake and blinks. (2.5s)
