# O97 Anora

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Right Now 2020–2025
- What it looks like: Pink, cyan and orange neon tubes glowing, their reflections dragging across the wet boardwalk and falling into the snow, with bloom, out-of-focus bokeh and grain. Anora walks from the Russian neon of Brighton Beach to the Vegas wedding signs — the whole sequence is lit by the tubes.
- Style: Snowy-night neon
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2024

## Style Prompt

Photographic 2D neon night. Real tubes: glass tube, glow, bright tube, white-hot core, clips, dark jumper wires; rare flicker (every 5–10 s). Neon pink #ff4fa8, cyan #4fdcff, orange #ff9a2e, green #3dff9a plus warm sodium light over a deep purple-blue night #1e1838. Put neon where the subject's world allows (shopfronts, a harbour bar, its own building). Wet ground fills the lower quarter: signs stretch into long vertical reflections broken by plank gaps, paving joints or ripples; snow falls and drifts at the edges, tinted by the spill. The subject keeps a warm key on the face (a lamp, a window, its own light), narrow neon rims on both sides; the distance melts into bokeh. Bloom, vignette, grain ±3%. Short 2–3 s shots, new angles on one place, each a slow push-in. Reference: Sean Baker, *Anora* (2024). Avoid: flat neon lettering, strobing.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Neon tube signs, wet-ground reflections and snow, 35mm night photography

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Anora: a cream fur earmuff hat, a raised fur collar, a big round earring. Four short shots walking on the beat, each with a little push and pull: the fireworks, the boardwalk with Vanya, the wedding sign, and finally the quiet car and the falling snow.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A long lens on the seaside Ferris wheel and parachute tower lit up, a New Year firework bursting; a slow push. (0.6s)
2. Cut to the snowy boardwalk, a row of Russian neon shop signs beside it: Clawd in its fur earmuff hat and collar standing in front, Vanya beside it laughing and waving, the signs reflected in the wet boards; the camera slowly pulling back. (0.7s)
3. Cut to Las Vegas: the heart-and-arrow 'Wedding' sign and the CHAPEL lightbox filling the frame, a small white chapel beside them; a slow push. (0.6s)
4. Cut to the last scene, inside a parked car: Clawd sitting on the dashboard, snow falling and settling on the windscreen behind it, Igor in the foreground holding the wheel; a slow push. (0.6s)
