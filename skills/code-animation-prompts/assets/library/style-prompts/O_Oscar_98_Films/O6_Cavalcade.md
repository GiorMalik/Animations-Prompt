# O6 Cavalcade

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Silent & Early Sound 1927–1938
- What it looks like: Mucha-style Art Nouveau decorative painting: swelling brown outlines, pastel flat colour, mosaic ground, a halo behind the central figure. The film opens on a family raising their glasses at midnight on New Year's Eve 1899 to welcome the new century — so the halo is made into a clock face striking twelve.
- Style: Mucha Art Nouveau decorative painting
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1932/33

## Style Prompt

Alphonse Mucha colour lithograph. Brown contours #4B3326, about 1/250 of frame width, doubling on the shadow side (light from upper left), tapered ends; pastel flats, each with a hard-edged darker shadow tone. Palette: cream #F5ECD6 paper/sky, sage #A9B490 foliage/drapery, pale gold #CFA55A halo/ornament, rose #DBA49C skin/flowers; the hero the story centres on in one stronger accent (e.g. #D97757). Wide frame: figure and a big ring-filled halo in the middle third, arched panels either side, behind them a muted-teal (sage plus grey) square-tile mosaic; whiplash vines, local flowers; a bottom band of serif capitals in the user's language, 1/25 of frame height. No plain area over 1/8 of frame. Almond eyes, heavy lids; hair or cloth in three-tone strands. Litho grain, paper mottle, vignette. Slow sway; tilt down to the halo, pull back. Avoid: thin clean lines, saturated colour, empty backgrounds.

### Director's notes

Clawd is one of the Marryot family raising a glass: silk top hat, white bow tie, champagne saucer in hand. On beat 3 the clock strikes twelve and the halo flashes, then a cut to the toast, and Clawd jumps and raises its glass. Plays 1:1, two hard cuts, the last pull-back settling on the whole poster.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Tilting down from the CAVALCADE title and the galloping-horse frieze to the clock-face halo and pushing in; the hands meet at XII, and the halo flashes as the clock strikes. (1.1s)
2. Mid-shot of the toast: Clawd in top hat and white bow tie jumps and raises its champagne saucer, Jane and Robert raising their glasses on either side; a gentle pull. (0.8s)
3. Pulling back to the whole Mucha decorative painting: three glasses raised, rose petals falling, and at the bottom the inscription band LONDON · XXXI DECEMBER MDCCCXCIX · A NEW CENTURY. (0.6s)
