# O90 The Shape of Water

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Indie Era 2010–2019
- What it looks like: Real-time 3D flooded bathroom and laboratory tank, everything immersed in teal-green: flowing caustics, beams of light falling from above, bubbles and a few warm lamps. Del Toro's film is soaked in exactly this water, so the whole sequence is shot underwater.
- Style: 3D underwater teal caustics
- Keywords: 3D
- Year: 2017

## Style Prompt

Real-time 3D, fully under water (land subjects sink into a flooded room or sunken place), in teal: background #041414, hemisphere light #5fb3a6 above and #0c2a24 below; teal fog, reds fading first with distance. A strong source above the surface projects moving caustics: bright cells about 1/20 of frame width on near surfaces, sliding over floor, walls and the subject. Shafts fan down; edges stay dark. Bubbles, motes, the rippled surface from below. Warm gold on 1–3 practicals, at least one large (a lit window or lamp ~1/10 of frame width), plus cyan bioluminescence. Smooth-shaded models, ambient occlusion, shallow focus, modest bloom, grain ±3%. Signature shot: low angle looking up, the subject half the frame height, off-centre beside the light; slow push-ins. Reference: Guillermo del Toro, *The Shape of Water* (2017). Avoid: flat water, caustics as sky stripes, tiny warm accents.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Real-time 3D (three.js), projected caustics and volumetric light beams, Dan Laustsen's teal-and-warm-gold grade

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Elisa, a deep-red velvet headband and bow on its head, later a hard-boiled egg in hand. 2.5 seconds, four cuts, the rhythm steady at 1.25×: the embrace under the light beam, the faces pressed together, the tank, and finally the two hands, the point of contact lighting up teal as the closing beat.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A low wide inside the flooded bathroom: the Amphibian Man holding Clawd in a beam of light, a red shoe sinking below; a slow push. (0.9s)
2. Cut to a close-up of the faces: his crown and gills pressed against a corner of Clawd, the red headband bow, bubbles rising; a slow push in. (0.5s)
3. Cut to the laboratory: a medium shot, Clawd standing before the glowing tank with a hard-boiled egg in hand, reaching back to press its hand to the glass; a slow push. (0.6s)
4. Cut to a close-up: two hands pressed together through the glass, a burst of teal light glowing at the point of contact. (0.4s)
