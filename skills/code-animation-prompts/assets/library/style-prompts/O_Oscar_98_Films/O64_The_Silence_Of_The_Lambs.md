# O64 The Silence of the Lambs

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Fin de Siècle 1990–1999
- What it looks like: Fine copperplate hatching and stipple, thinly hand-coloured, with plate numbers and Latin labels printed on yellowed paper. The death's-head moth on the poster belongs pinned to a specimen plate, and the same cool copperplate manner carries into Lecter's cell.
- Style: Hand-coloured natural-history copperplate
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1991

## Style Prompt

A hand-coloured natural-history copperplate. Every object in engraved warm black-brown line: dense parallel and cross-hatching (spacing about 1/400 of frame width, thicker in darks), stipple for soft tones, hatched shadows beneath; sky bare or sparsely lined. Flat watercolour tints inside the line, medium saturation, clearly coloured: paper #EFE3C4, umber #5A3A22, rust #B4431E, yellow #E6AD24, grey-violet #7D6F8E. Plate: the subject as Fig. 1 in the centre, about 60% of plate height, 3–6 numbered part or object figures around it, each about 1/5 high; spaced small-caps title, numbered legend, bevelled plate mark, light foxing. Even daylight; time of day only shifts the tints' warmth; dark interiors go cold, hard side light. Camera: a still plate where one thing moves (insect, leaf, hand), then a slow push to a key detail. Reference: Moses Harris, *The Aurelian* (1766). Avoid: smooth gradients; garish colour; a tiny vignetted subject.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Copperplate engraving hatching, hand-colouring, Victorian entomology plates, Moses Harris's The Aurelian

### Director's notes

Clawd is first 'Plate 4' on the specimen plate, then plays Clarice with her FBI trainee badge. The moth lands in the first few beats; a hard cut to the cell, and the push toward Lecter runs in one unbroken movement with no cut, his smile saved for the very end. Scene time is about 1.8× screen time.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. The whole entomology plate 'Acherontia styx': a live death's-head moth takes off from a nightshade plant and lands on Clawd's head; a gentle push. (0.9s)
2. A hard cut to the underground cell block: Clawd as Clarice with her FBI trainee badge standing before the glass; then one continuous push to the right and inward until Lecter's face fills the frame, the corner of his mouth showing the faintest smile. (1.6s)
