# O2 The Broadway Melody

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Silent & Early Sound 1927–1938
- What it looks like: A moving black-gold-silver Art Deco musical poster: airbrushed gradients, a stepped proscenium, a chrome condenser microphone. 1929 Broadway was at the height of Art Deco, and this film is one of the ones that brought sound — so the sound waves ripple out of the microphone in rings.
- Style: Black-gold-silver Art Deco musical poster
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1928/29

## Style Prompt

Art Deco revue poster. Black lacquer ground; gold and silver from banded metallic gradients (3-5 hard bands); airbrush glows, sunbursts, ziggurat steps, streamlined curves; a stepped proscenium frame of gold lines and bulbs. Palette: ground #0A0908, gold #D6B560, highlight #F3E2A2, silver #BFC4C9; skin pale silver-cream; one saturated accent (e.g. #D97757) only on the hero the story centres on. Elongated Erté-style figures with real faces; fringe, sequins or feathers where the subject allows. Fine grain, glints, stars. Frontal symmetry, a central spotlight. Motion: chasing bulbs, a row of repeated elements (dancers, waves, columns) rippling toward the centre, rings radiating from the main sound or light source; slow pushes, cut on the music's beat (without music, every 2-3 s). References: A. M. Cassandre's posters, Erté's costumes. Avoid: flat yellow for metal, faceless figures, extra hues.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Art Deco poster, airbrushed metal gradients, A. M. Cassandre, Erté costumes

### Director's notes

Clawd is a tap dancer in a boater hat with a black cane, tapping from start to finish. It fades in from the white-out of part 1; on beat 4 the microphone comes alive and sound waves pour out (sound film has arrived). Four hard cuts: street corner → stage → microphone → chorus line → Clawd.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Fading in from a white-out: the Astor Theatre's marquee (THE BROADWAY MELODY / ALL TALKING · ALL SINGING · ALL DANCING), bulbs chasing; Clawd in a boater hat with a cane stands on the left; the camera pulls back slowly. (0.8s)
2. Wide of the stage: Art Deco proscenium, chorus girls on the steps, Clawd tap-dancing beside a giant condenser microphone as it starts rippling out sound waves in rings; the camera pulls back slowly. (1s)
3. Close-up of the chrome spring-ring microphone, sound waves radiating, notes flying out; the camera pushes in. (0.5s)
4. Tracking along the kicking line: feather headdresses, sequinned skirts, legs kicking in waves row by row. (0.5s)
5. Close-up of Clawd, its boater tipping side to side as it taps, the microphone ring behind it; a gentle push. (0.5s)
