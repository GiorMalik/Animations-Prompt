# O81 Slumdog Millionaire

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · New Millennium 2000–2009
- What it looks like: A TV quiz-show studio in blue-violet with gold highlights, moving-head light beams, lens flares; then a cut to saturated orange-teal Mumbai rooftops. The film itself cuts back and forth between the quiz show's final question and the slum Jamal comes from.
- Style: Quiz-show studio + Mumbai orange-teal
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2008

## Style Prompt

Two registers. Primary, about 70%: a glossy illustrated quiz-show studio, bright and full, seen three-quarter from slightly above: a glass stage lit from below with concentric blue rings, a packed audience behind, moving-head beams cutting blue-violet #5A3FD0 through haze, a big screen of elongated-hexagon answers, one locked in amber #F0A020; bloom and horizontal flares; as tension rises the edges dim to about half, never black. Characters rounded, softly shaded. Second register, about 30%, as full-frame hard cuts: golden-hour views of the subject's home ground, saturated orange and teal, busy (e.g. tin roofs, washing lines, crowds). Accents: magenta #E2206B, teal #0F7C7A, tangerine #F06A14. Camera: push in on screen and hot seat as beams close on one person; elsewhere slow pans. Reference: cinematographer Anthony Dod Mantle (*Slumdog Millionaire*, 2008). Avoid: real show logos; a mostly black studio; even lighting; grey, empty places.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

TV quiz-show lighting, blue-violet beams and lens flares, Anthony Dod Mantle's orange-teal grade

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Jamal: a light-blue fine-checked shirt collar and a lavalier mic. The answer is locked from the very first frame; the second shot dims the whole studio and closes in on the hot seat, then opens up again as the answer turns green; then a hard cut to the Mumbai rooftop, the frame opening out to the whole city.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Close-up: the final question on the big screen, four answer boxes, A: ARAMIS locked in amber; a slight push. No Clawd. (0.8s)
2. Wide of the stage: the host sitting on a stool, Clawd as Jamal in the hot seat; the whole studio darkens, the beams closing in on Clawd, then the answer flashes green and the audience cheers; a slow push. (1s)
3. Wide: the Dharavi rooftops at golden hour, Clawd standing on a corrugated roof, a crowded train passing on the elevated line behind; the camera slowly pans right. (0.8s)
