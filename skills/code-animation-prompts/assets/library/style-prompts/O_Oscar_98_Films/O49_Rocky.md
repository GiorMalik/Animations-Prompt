# O49 Rocky

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · New Hollywood 1970–1979
- What it looks like: All of Philadelphia at dawn built in 3D, from the meat locker to the museum's 72 steps, graded to 1970s 35mm: cold blue sky, a low golden sun, long shadows, thin mist, fine grain. Running up the steps and the circling crane shot at the top are the film's signature moments.
- Style: Philadelphia steps sunrise 3D · 35mm grade
- Keywords: 3D
- Year: 1976

## Style Prompt

1970s 35mm realism in a detailed 3D world, sunrise by default (sunset the same). Sun 5–10° up, warm orange #F0A060, lighting the hero from the side or three-quarter front so the lit body and long ground shadows read; no full backlight. Cool blue sky #7FA3CF, pink cloud undersides #E6B2AE; haze golden toward the sun, blue-violet away. Grade: fine grain (±3%), cool shadows, warm highlights, nothing clipped; flare and ghosts only with the sun in frame. Camera: a low Steadicam following the hero from behind up an ascent, then at the top one continuous orbit that circles, rises and pulls back over 6–8 s, showing the lit face, until the place spreads out below. References: John G. Avildsen, *Rocky* (1976); Steadicam operator Garrett Brown. Avoid: flat noon light, detail-less silhouettes, a static climax, plastic CG shine.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Rocky: a grey hooded sweatshirt with the hood up, a white towel over its shoulder. The training montage is three fast cuts, about 1.7×; once on the steps it returns to 1×. A hard cut into the Steadicam tracking run, then no more cuts: reaching the top it goes straight into the circling crane shot, the arms-raised jump landing just as the camera starts to rise.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Mid-shot in the meat locker, locked-off camera: Clawd in its grey hood punching the hanging sides of beef, white tiles, a bare bulb. (0.4s)
2. Closer still, locked-off camera: fists hitting the beef, the beef swaying. (0.4s)
3. The Italian Market before dawn, locked-off camera: Clawd jogging toward the camera past barrel fires, fruit stalls and streetlights still lit. (0.6s)
4. A low Steadicam shot chasing Clawd from behind up the 72 steps, the museum's columns ahead. (1.5s)
5. At the top, Clawd jumps with both arms raised in a V; the camera circles around it, rises and pulls back until the whole avenue and city spread out in the sunrise. (2.1s)
