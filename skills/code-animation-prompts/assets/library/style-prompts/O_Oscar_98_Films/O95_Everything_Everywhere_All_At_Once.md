# O95 Everything Everywhere All at Once

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Right Now 2020–2025
- What it looks like: Four universes, four drawing styles, all real-time raymarching: the cold-green IRS, the hot-dog-finger piano under advertising light, the silent canyon of googly-eyed rocks, the everything bagel turned black hole. Just like the film, the googly-eye stickers jump between universes.
- Style: Raymarched multiverse jump cuts
- Keywords: 3D
- Year: 2022

## Style Prompt

Ray-marched real-time 3D multiverse montage: the subject jumps through 3–4 universes, each its own look, invented from its world, e.g. a realistic workplace under cold green fluorescents, densely dressed; a surreal cosmic version with a gravitational-lens halo; a silent wide landscape with white subtitles; a candy-pink product shot with pink-and-cyan rims. Each holds 2–4 s with its own grade. One link through all: a googly-eye sticker on the subject, about 1/10 of frame height. Transition, 0.5–0.8 s: push into the eye, smear, split RGB, then a rainbow ring of colour segments (#e5322d, #f7c928, #1fa39a, #2a6fc0, #e0478f) studded with googly eyes and pink beads blooms to ~80% of frame height and bursts to white. Signature frame: the ring around the subject; first jump at ~1 s. Reference: Daniels, *Everything Everywhere All at Once* (2022). Avoid: look-alike universes, a small plain ring.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

GLSL raymarched multiple universes, one style per universe (realist office, surreal universe, landscape, advertisement), googly-eye rainbow mandala

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Evelyn, a googly eye stuck to its forehead, a fanny pack at its waist. Every universe jump starts with a push into that eye, then motion trails, RGB separation and a white flash, landing on the cut; each universe gets a different action: standing, playing piano keys with its feet, sitting with the rocks, being sucked into the bagel. At the end it returns to the start, opening its arms inside the mandala.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Wide of the IRS office, receipts flying everywhere, the camera pushing down the aisle toward Clawd; a googly eye stuck to its forehead, a fanny pack at its waist. (0.8s)
2. Cut to the auditor and her steel desk piled with receipts, holding up one receipt; a slow push. (0.6s)
3. Cut to Clawd with its arms raised, the camera rushing into the googly eye on its forehead, the universe-jump flash bursting open. (0.4s)
4. The hot-dog-finger universe: a wide of the glossy piano under candy-pink light, a hand's hot-dog fingers flopped softly on the keys, Clawd standing on the piano keys. (0.5s)
5. Cut close to Clawd's feet, pressing the keys one by one, little hot dogs growing at the ends of its arms too. (0.5s)
6. Another push into the googly eye, into the next universe jump. (0.4s)
7. Wide beside the silent canyon: two rocks with googly eyes stuck on, Clawd sitting quietly beside them, the subtitle 'We're all small and stupid.'; the camera drifting slowly along the cliff edge. (1s)
8. The everything bagel: Clawd looking up at the giant black bagel, the camera pushing all the way into the black hole in its centre, crossing the event horizon, Clawd tilting forward as it is pulled in. (1.7s)
9. Back in the office: Clawd opening its arms, a ring of rainbow mandala with googly eyes growing behind it; the camera gently pulling back. (0.6s)
