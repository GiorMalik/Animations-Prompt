# O87 Birdman

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Indie Era 2010–2019
- What it looks like: A detailed 3D Broadway theatre backstage, shot with a handheld long take: warm tungsten light against cold fluorescent light, ending in the blazing light of Times Square. Birdman's most famous trick is pretending to be one continuous shot, so the sequence's style is that camera move.
- Style: 3D backstage corridor long take
- Keywords: 3D
- Year: 2014

## Style Prompt

Real-time 3D, one unbroken handheld long take, after cinematographer Emmanuel Lubezki (*Birdman*, 2014). A narrow, lived-in passage (default a backstage corridor; an outdoor subject gets its nearest interior, e.g. a stairwell): chipped blood-red enamel dado #6e2a22 on the lower third, stained nicotine plaster #cdb98e above, pipes, cable trays, doors, pinned notices. Light only from practicals every 2–3 m: warm caged tungsten bulbs, one cold flickering fluorescent tube, a red exit sign #ff2a1a; the far door glows. Mid-key: plaster light near lamps, never below mid-grey between them; floor visible. Wide lens (18–24 mm), slow sway (±1°), soft ambient occlusion, light bloom, fine grain (±3%). Over 20–40 s, in any order: lead the subject walking backwards, whip aside to a doorway, let it brush past the lens, turn and follow. Avoid: a locked-off camera, blank walls, repeated textures, a crushed dark frame.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Real-time 3D (three.js), Lubezki's handheld long take, tungsten and fluorescent light, digital cinema fine grain

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Riggan, wearing a slate-blue Birdman helmet, a gold beak, wings at the sides. The corridor is one fast handheld camera move, the drummer's swinging drumbeat inside the frame; pushing the back door open lands on the beat, cutting to Times Square, where Clawd stops and looks up.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A handheld medium shot, the camera walking backward in front of Clawd in its Birdman helmet, whipping left to the drummer playing jazz in a small room, then whipping back, Clawd walking past the drum kit and brushing past the lens. (1.3s)
2. A cut to the back door, shot from behind Clawd, a red EXIT light above; the camera shifting gently sideways as Clawd pushes the door open onto a wash of white light. (0.7s)
3. A cut to Times Square at night: a wide, Clawd standing on the pavement with a wall of LED screens behind; it takes one step and looks up at the screens, the handheld camera drifting gently. (0.5s)
