# O36 Tom Jones

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Swinging Sixties 1960–1969
- What it looks like: A Georgian satirical print: Hogarth-style etched lines that swell and thin, with directional hatching, brushed over with Rowlandson-style bright hand-colouring that spills slightly past the lines, plus one speech balloon. The film itself is imitating Hogarth's 18th-century England.
- Style: Hand-coloured satirical copper engraving
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1963

## Style Prompt

Georgian satirical print: a copperplate engraving, hand-coloured. Brown-black line only: swelling burin outlines about 1/400 of frame width, heavier on the shadow side; hatching follows each form and covers every surface (spacing about 1/250 of frame width), cross-hatched in the darks, ruled on flat areas. Colour covers nearly the whole picture, clear but strong: washes spilling a few pixels off the line in claret #B3262E, brass #D8A93A, flesh #F0C4A4, slate blue #6D8AA6, warm ochre and umber on large surfaces. Sepia laid paper, foxing, plate mark, engraved border. Stage it like William Hogarth: big-nosed caricatures, bystanders or comic props and animals around the subject; one slanted handwritten speech balloon in the user's language. Camera: hold the full plate 2 s, whip in, glide to another detail, close-up. Avoid: even mechanical hatching, blank paper, colour exactly inside lines, muddy colour.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Tom Jones: a gold-trimmed tricorn, a lipstick kiss mark on its cheek, a chicken leg in hand. No shot-reverse-shot — one slide instead: gliding from the two-shot across the laden table to Mrs Waters winking as a speech balloon appears. Then a close-up: heart eyes, a rapturous wobble, and it takes the tricorn off and tips it toward the camera, the picture going black, with the next film opening out of the black.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Whipping in from the left to a two-shot across the table full of dishes, Clawd as Tom tearing into a chicken leg; the camera slides across the table to Mrs Waters, who produces a speech balloon (Another Leg, Mr Jones?) and winks. (1.3s)
2. Close-up of Clawd: its eyes turn into hearts, it wobbles rapturously, takes another small bite, then takes off its tricorn and tips it toward the camera as the picture goes black; a slow push in. (1.2s)
