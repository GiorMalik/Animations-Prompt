# O53 Ordinary People

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Blockbuster Era 1980–1989
- What it looks like: Transparent watercolour in layered glazes: wet-on-wet sky and lake, tidemark edges, backruns, the white paper left as the highlights, autumn gold and rust red against cold grey-blue. It matches the film's opening — a quiet, slightly melancholy Lake Forest autumn.
- Style: Autumn transparent watercolour
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1980

## Style Prompt

Transparent watercolour in layered glazes on cold-press paper. Draw shapes crisply first, then two or three glazes, each smaller and darker, drying to a tide-mark edge; two or three blooms, about 1/10 of frame width, inside large washes, never on figures; light granulation (±4%). Paper is the brightest white; light objects and highlights left as paper. Sky and water wet-in-wet, ripples dry-brush. Roles: autumn gold #ECAA34, scarlet #E46030, rust #B8743C for foliage and accents, against grey-blue mist #C2C6D2 and violet-grey shadows #8A8496; sky pale and cool at any time of day; ground pale green-ochre. Foliage: pale wash, scallop-edged clusters, dark dots, flicked leaves; with few trees, warm colours go to scrub, roofs, clothes. Light from one side. Camera: slow pan, gentle push, leaves drifting past the lens. Reference: Winslow Homer's watercolours. Avoid: opaque gouache, airbrushed blur, vector edges, muddy colour, saturated sunsets.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Transparent watercolour glazing, Winslow Homer and John Singer Sargent, HB pencil underdrawing

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Conrad: a long navy-and-cream school scarf lifted by the lake wind, a leather book bag on its back. The whole thing at 1.1×, as slow as the Pachelbel Canon opening. Three hard cuts: the lake, the son looking back at the house, the mother who does not respond. Its gaze dropping from the house to the ground is the whole performance.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. An empty shot of Lake Michigan at dawn: a white-paper path of sunlight left on the grey-blue lake, the camera panning slowly right to the autumn shore. (0.8s)
2. A street under the maples: Clawd as Conrad in its long striped scarf and book bag stops on the pavement and looks back at the white colonial house (Calvin raking leaves in the yard), then lowers its eyes and turns away; the camera drifts slowly right and pulls back slightly. (1s)
3. Beth on the porch, arms crossed, her expression cold; the camera slowly pushes in on her. (0.7s)
