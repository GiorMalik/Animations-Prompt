# O5 Grand Hotel

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Silent & Early Sound 1927–1938
- What it looks like: A round lobby built in three.js, graded in MGM's silvery black and white, with Clawd's orange as the only colour. This film is most famous for the shot looking straight down from the top, the galleries spiralling inward — so the whole set is built around that one shot.
- Style: MGM silvery B&W 3D lobby
- Keywords: 3D·B&W
- Year: 1931/32

## Style Prompt

A real 3D set, by default an Art Deco interior (marble, chrome, chandelier, glossy checkerboard floor); outdoors keep the real setting with glossy surfaces (wet pavement, still water) and symmetrical framing. Ambient occlusion, soft bloom; 1930s studio silver: about 40% deep black, glossy whites near 90%, no washed-out greys. Light falls in shafts from one high source. All neutral grey except the hero the story centres on (e.g. #D97757), at least 1/6 of frame height; warm lamps render white, bloom only around them. Grain (±3%), halation. Signature shot: eye-height wide into a deep symmetrical set, floor and ceiling in frame, floor reflections; then 4-6 s tracks; one overhead on rings, figures as chess pieces. Period figures (heads, hats, limbs) walk routes. References: art director Cedric Gibbons, cinematographer William Daniels. Avoid: walking in place, frame-filling abstract patterns, colour casts.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

three.js 3D, MGM silvery B&W, Art Deco interior, top-down concentric composition

### Director's notes

Clawd plays the bellboy, in a pillbox cap, carrying a suitcase covered in hotel labels. It really walks: 1.4 metres per second, 2.4 steps, steps matched to the distance covered. The passage plays 1:1; at 1.08 s one cut takes it from the top-down shot to a side tracking shot.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Looking straight down from the skylight: six floors of galleries spiralling in toward the key tower, the orange bellboy a single dot on the chequered marble, walking across the floor; the camera pushes in slowly. (1.1s)
2. A side tracking shot at Clawd's height: it walks forward with the suitcase, the lobby sliding past behind it, the revolving door turning ahead; the camera tracks forward and in. (1.4s)
