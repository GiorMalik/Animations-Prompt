# O25 The Greatest Show on Earth

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Widescreen 1950–1959
- What it looks like: A Strobridge circus colour lithograph: chrome yellow, vermilion and cobalt blue flat colour, thick black outlines, with halftone dots, misregistration and litho grain. DeMille's big top is drawn as three poster rings, with Clawd as Sebastian doing the aerial catch.
- Style: Strobridge circus colour lithograph
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1952

## Style Prompt

A circus chromolithograph after the Strobridge Lithographing Co. (c. 1890). Flat ink, heavy black outlines (about 1/300 of frame width), crescent shadows, light from upper left. Four stones: chrome yellow #F4C416, vermilion #D62C24, cobalt #1E4EA0, key black #181210 on warm cream paper #EBD9B4; overprints and tints give only orange, green, brown, tan, pale blue, grey. Solids print flat; screens (dots about 1/250 of frame width) only on gradients (sky, skin, ground), each stone at its own angle, 1-3 px off register, stone grain. The hero the story centres on is solid yellow under solid vermilion, a flat orange (e.g. #D97757). Exaggerated perspective, radial lines converging on the action, several actions at once; any crowd is tiny individual figures, though a lone figure works. Camera: tilt up, lateral dolly, cut on action. Avoid: off-palette colours, dot-only crowds, a screened, pinkish hero.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Four-colour lithography, Strobridge circus posters, flat inks + thick black outlines

### Director's notes

Clawd plays the Great Sebastian: a sequinned cape with a gold scalloped edge, hanging upside down from the trapeze. Tilting up finds it on the trapeze bar; the elephant tracking shot in the middle keeps the parade moving; the last cut lands mid-action on the catch, timed to the trapeze swinging right, matching the poster's composition, as it flings its arm toward the audience.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Wide of the big top: three performance rings, bowl-shaped stands, elephants on the track; tilting up along the tent to Clawd hanging upside down from the highest trapeze. (0.8s)
2. Tracking the elephant parade sideways: each one holding the tail of the one ahead, sequinned dancers riding on their backs, the ring fence and the backs of front-row heads in the foreground. (0.6s)
3. Cut mid-action to the catch: Clawd in its sequinned cape, hanging by its knees, catches the girl flyer in pink with one hand and flings the other toward the audience as the trapeze swings them to the right; the camera pushes slightly and tilts a little. (1s)
