# O23 All About Eve

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Widescreen 1950–1959
- What it looks like: Al Hirschfeld's Broadway caricatures: flowing ink lines that swell and thin, black blocks against white, lots of empty space, each person caught by a single exaggerated feature. The film is about the theatre, so the party is drawn as a newspaper theatre-page caricature; Clawd is the only colour.
- Style: Hirschfeld one-line caricature
- Keywords: 2D·B&W
- Year: 1950

## Style Prompt

An Al Hirschfeld-style caricature. Lines are elastic, long and continuous, one stroke per contour: about 1/800 of frame width at the ends, swelling to 1/250 on curves. Dark clothing, hair and big objects go solid black; grey only as sparse parallel hatching. The setting is fully drawn in thin line, architecture, furniture and props in detail, white kept between objects: about 60% paper white, never an empty page. Push one trait per figure (a long neck, a big head). Paper #FBFAF6, ink #131012; only the hero the story centres on has its body or main garment filled flat with one colour (e.g. #D97757) instead of black. Lines write on within the first second, then hold; a short hand-lettered line may sit in the white. Camera: ease back, pan across, push in. Avoid: stiff limbs, stick figures, a bare setting.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

One-line ink caricature, Al Hirschfeld theatre caricatures, handwritten dialogue

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Margo: a row of black short curls, a string of pearls, a martini, hand resting on the banister. The first shot is the picture being drawn and the dialogue written in; one pan finds Eve in the corner, watching her; after the last hard cut, Margo raises her glass on the beat. The performance runs at 1.8×.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A wide of Margo's party drawn line by line: first the staircase, then the guests and the piano; the line 'FASTEN YOUR SEATBELTS, IT'S GOING TO BE A BUMPY NIGHT' is handwritten into the empty space, and Clawd as Margo stands on the stairs; the camera pulls back slightly. (1.1s)
2. Panning right across the guests: Bertie, Max, Addison, Miss Caswell, the pianist, stopping on Eve in the corner. (0.8s)
3. Close-up of Clawd on the stairs: black curls, pearl necklace, raising its martini; a gentle push. (0.7s)
