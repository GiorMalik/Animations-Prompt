# O17 Going My Way

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Colour & War 1939–1949
- What it looks like: Backlit Gothic stained glass: leaded panes of coloured glass, faces drawn in grey tones, Chartres blue and jewel-red borders, coloured light spilling onto the church floor. Father O'Malley and the choir are set into the window, with a gold star above them.
- Style: Backlit Gothic stained-glass window
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1944

## Style Prompt

A backlit Gothic stained-glass window filling 80-90% of frame height and ~60% of width, neighbours glowing at edges. Every shape is cut glass: lightness varied ±8% per piece, streaks, seed bubbles, a brown edge mat, black lead (about 1/250 of frame width). Sky and empty ground: diamond quarries (about 1/30 of frame width); the border is ruby with beads. The main figure is at least half the window's height, in dark-brown grisaille line. Palette by role: background cobalt #1B3F9E, border ruby #B3121F, highlights gold #E3A526, lead #16161A; skin pale amber; scenery deep greens. Only the hero the story centres on is orange glass (e.g. #D97757). Bright glass blooms into the leads; coloured light spills onto floor and sill. Camera: tilt down, pan, settle on the pooled light. References: Chartres Cathedral; Harry Clarke. Avoid: flat fills, random cuts, dark empty wall.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Leaded stained glass + grey-tone painting, Chartres blue diamond lattice, Harry Clarke jewel colours

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Father O'Malley in the glass painting: its body is the warmest terracotta glass, in a straw hat and Roman collar, holding a baton and a hymn book. It conducts to the beat, the boys' mouths each slightly out of sync. One tilt down from the star to Clawd, then a hard cut to the choir, then down to the coloured light on the floor. The performance runs at 1.8×.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Starting on the gold eight-pointed star and the 'SWINGING ON A STAR' ribbon, tilting down the pointed-arch window to Clawd as Father O'Malley, conducting the boys' choir inside the glass. (1.3s)
2. Mid-shot of the glass choir: boys in red robes and white surplices singing, the camera panning right past Clawd's raised baton hand. (0.6s)
3. Cut to the nave: the stained glass's colours spilling across the marble aisle between the pews, altar candles burning; the camera tilts down to the floor. (0.6s)
