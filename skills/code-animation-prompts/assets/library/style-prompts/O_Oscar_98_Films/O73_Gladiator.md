# O73 Gladiator

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · New Millennium 2000–2009
- What it looks like: The whole picture laid in square stone and glass tesserae: the stones along the outlines follow the forms, the flat areas run in straight rows, and the gold-leaf glass pieces glint. The film is set in Rome, so the Colosseum and the Elysian wheat field are both made as Roman floor mosaics.
- Style: Roman floor mosaic
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2000

## Style Prompt

Roman floor mosaic: over a flat underdrawing lay square tesserae about 1/150 of frame width: two to three courses follow each outline (opus vermiculatum), flat areas in straight rows, figures outlined with one course of dark stones. Muted, warm stone and glass: burnt gold #E2B24C, umber #5B4332, terracotta #8E3C2B, warm marble #E9DDC4, with imperial-purple glass for one accent only; sky and water in pale grey-blue and cream stone, never saturated bands. Tesserae slightly irregular, lit from the upper left, shadowing into dark 1–2 px grout; gold-leaf glass glints slowly on light sources. Frontal, slightly stiff Pompeian figures before a faded field of fine stones. Short pushes and pulls; motion from details (a gesture, a crowd, plants, a light shaft). References: *Alexander Mosaic*, Zliten mosaic. Avoid: a neat grid that reads as pixel art; oversized tiles; smooth gradients; bright grout.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Stones laid following the forms (opus vermiculatum), gold-leaf glass tesserae, the Zliten gladiator mosaic

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Maximus: one bronze shoulder guard, a Roman gladius. The opening is a thumbs-down; a hard cut to the Colosseum, the crowd rising around Clawd; the last cut leaves the fighting and lands on the quiet wheat field of the afterlife. Three hard cuts, each about a second.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Close-up: the purple imperial box, Commodus's thumb turning from up to down; a slight push. (0.8s)
2. A poster-style wide of the Colosseum: Clawd as Maximus standing at the centre, gladius pointed down, a chained tiger at the left and Tigris at the right, the crowd in the stands rising; the camera pulls slightly. (1s)
3. Wide of the afterlife: under a low sun, Clawd standing in a golden wheat field, cypress trees and a farmhouse on the horizon; a slow push. (0.8s)
