- What it looks like: A soldier's field diary: pencil lines that vary in weight, transparent watercolour, yellowed rag paper, iron-gall ink handwriting and pencil notes in the margins. Dunbar writes and draws in his diary throughout the film, so the prairie comes alive out of his sketches.

## Style Prompt

A 19th-century field journal come to life: full-colour watercolour over visible pencil. Pencil under-drawing #3A363A, pressure-varied and broken, lightly hatched in shadow, readable through the washes. Brushed washes with soft wet edges, blooms, pooled rims and granulation (not flat polygons) cover about 80% of the frame in rich warm colour. Default magic hour: dusty-rose sky #C88F76 turning lavender above, streaky rose clouds, low sun, ochre-gold land #DEBE87, violet distance, long shadows, opaque pale-gold lifts on sun-facing edges. Yellowed rag paper #F0E4C8, faint stains and foxing, showing through. Iron-gall ink #34221C for small labels and arrows. Signature move, once at the opening (about 2 s): bare pencil, then colour arrives wash by wash, far to near, during a push-in; after that, full colour. References: Bodmer and Catlin field sketches. Avoid: grey, muddy or pale washes; uniform digital outlines; later pencil-only frames.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Pencil underdrawing, transparent watercolour, iron-gall ink handwriting, Bodmer and Catlin field watercolours

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Dunbar: a black cavalry slouch hat with a gold hat cord, a diary in one hand and a pencil in the other. Pushing into the page and the colouring is one movement; the cut to the prairie come alive lands mid-phrase, and the hat tip lands on the last few beats. 1:1 time.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Pushing into the diary's right-hand page: a self-portrait of Clawd and the wolf 'Two Socks' starts as pencil underdrawing, watercolour washing on stroke by stroke — the sky first, and Clawd last. (1.2s)
2. Cut to the sunset prairie in full colour: one push from the plain to Clawd and Two Socks facing each other, a buffalo herd thundering past behind; Clawd tips its hat. (1.3s)
