# O8 Mutiny on the Bounty

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Silent & Early Sound 1927–1938
- What it looks like: An 18th-century British Admiralty chart as a copper engraving: rhumb lines, compass roses, depth soundings, hand tinting, with the Bounty as a ship illustration on the chart. The film tells of one voyage, so the whole passage stays on the chart.
- Style: Admiralty chart copper engraving
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1935

## Style Prompt

A late-18th-century copperplate map, default an Admiralty sea chart (inland: a survey map). Fine burin hatching and cross-hatching (lines ~1/1200 of frame width) give all tone; a minimum line width makes small detail darken, not vanish. Ink #1C1610 on laid paper #EFE3C4, light foxing, folds. Thin hand colour: water #9EBCBC along edges, land #B8C08C; the hero the story centres on (e.g. #D97757) is the one strong colour. Rhumb lines from 32-point roses, a graduated border, hachured hills, cartouche, one ornament (e.g. a sea monster), serif lettering. Put the action in an unboxed engraved vignette fading into the chart, filling 60-75% of frame height at the end of the move. Camera: one 5-6 s glide from the whole sheet into the vignette, chart still around it; a dotted route inks itself in. Avoid: cropping the paper, heavy washes, stiff figures.

### Technique keywords

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Fletcher Christian: bicorne worn sideways, cutlass in hand. The passage never leaves the chart — one shot slides in, no cuts. On beat 4 it raises the cutlass, then the blade flashes toward the launch; in the last beats the launch drifts away toward the stern.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. One continuous shot: starting on the whole chart, the Bounty advances along its dashed track, the camera sliding into the ship illustration on the chart, rhumb lines and compass roses all around; on deck Clawd raises its cutlass, pointing Bligh into the boat, the launch drops to the stern and sails off, its track to Timor drawn across the chart. (2.5s)
