## Style Prompt

Photoreal-leaning 3D landscape at dusk. Sky fills 60% of the frame, dusty ochre #D8A060 at the horizon up to pale cream, thin streaked clouds lit from below. Sun 1–2° above the horizon, still casting shadows; its light burns along the far ridge. Low saturation: hundreds of grey-green scrub clumps receding to the horizon, stones, dry grass, dark warm earth #2A2622 (default arid; elsewhere keep sparse clumped growth). The hero stands backlit in the mid-ground, about a third of frame height, long shadow toward the lens. Expose for the sky: the sun may clip, its glow within a tenth of the frame. Grade: neutral tone mapping, warm highlights, fine grain, vignette. Signature shot: locked, low, wide (~35 mm). Reference: cinematographer Roger Deakins (*No Country for Old Men*, 2007). Avoid: saturated orange sunsets; blown-out glare; bare ground; busy camera moves.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Llewelyn: a light-brown felt cowboy hat, a leather briefcase. Three locked-off cameras hard-cut: walking with the case at sunset, the 'call it' coin in the middle, and headlights bearing down at night — danger arriving. The light carries the time from dusk to dark.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A low backlit wide: Clawd as Llewelyn in its cowboy hat, carrying the money case over a small rise, the sun pressed to the horizon at its left, a shot-up pickup behind it; locked-off camera. (0.8s)
2. Macro: a 25-cent coin spinning on a gas-station counter, Chigurh's belt and hand out of focus behind; the coin wobbling faster and faster, then falling; locked-off camera. No Clawd. (0.8s)
3. Night, a low camera close to the empty highway: Clawd standing on the shoulder, a pickup's headlights bearing down from the crest of the hill; locked-off camera. (0.8s)
