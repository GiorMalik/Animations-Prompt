# O51 The Deer Hunter

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · New Hollywood 1970–1979
- What it looks like: Andrew Wyeth-style egg tempera: dry, fine cross-hatched short strokes, low-saturation straw yellow, ochre brown and grey-green, each blade of grass drawn, a pale sky. Wyeth was a Pennsylvania painter, and the 'one shot' deer hunt takes place in the hills above a Pennsylvania steel town.
- Style: Andrew Wyeth egg tempera
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1978

## Style Prompt

Egg tempera on gesso panel after Andrew Wyeth. Dry, matte, very fine: colour built from dense cross-hatched strokes, each about 1/400 of frame width long, hairline thin. Muted palette: pale overcast sky #D9D5C6, straw grass #CDB27A, umber #6A5234, misty grey-green hills #8E938C, rust #8A4E32; water, stone, clothes and skin mixed from these. One accent colour, only on the one hero subject (e.g. a red cap); any light source is a small pale dot, no halo. Ground (dry grass by default, other ground alike): big light and dark patches, fine strokes, dark tufts, then single wind-combed blades; rocks, buildings hatched along their planes. Empty, lonely space; the subject small, off-centre. Soft overcast light; at other times of day stay pale, no warm glow. Camera: slow drift, quiet push; a circular iris may mark a cut. Avoid: wet watercolour blooms, saturated colour, thick shiny strokes.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Gesso-ground tempera, Andrew Wyeth, fine cross-hatched short strokes

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Mike: a red wool hunting cap, a scoped rifle. River valley → scope → deer's eye → hunter, four hard cuts, averaging about 1.24×, quiet and steady. The performance beat is the shot not taken: the rifle lowered, eyes closed, a breath out, then looking up at the deer.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Wide of a steel town in the fog of the river valley: blast furnaces, smoking chimneys, an onion-domed church; the camera pans slowly. (0.6s)
2. Through the scope: a black circular mask and crosshairs around the buck on the ridge; the picture sways gently with the hunter's hand. (0.7s)
3. Close-up of the buck's head and eye; a slow push. (0.3s)
4. Clawd as Mike standing on the granite, lowering its rifle, closing its eyes to breathe out, then looking up toward the buck; the camera pulls back to the whole ridge. (0.8s)
