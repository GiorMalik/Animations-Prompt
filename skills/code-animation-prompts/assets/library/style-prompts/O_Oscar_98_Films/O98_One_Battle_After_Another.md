# O98 One Battle After Another

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Right Now 2020–2025
- What it looks like: Real-time 3D desert highway, rising and falling like a roller coaster, three cars chasing over slope after slope, shot on a super-telephoto with heat haze, bleached glare and fine grain. Recreating the film's most famous sequence: the undulating highway chase.
- Style: 3D undulating highway chase · telephoto compression
- Keywords: 3D
- Year: 2025

## Style Prompt

Real-time 3D, bleached desert daylight. Signature shot (3–5 s): a straight road over rolling dips and crests at a 2–5° horizontal field of view, stacking dips into waves; the travelling subject sinks and reappears; sky a thin band at most. Then 1–2 s each, a low camera at a crest, a mount on the vehicle or rider. Palette: sky #7fb0e0, haze #ece4d2 only at the far horizon, pale sand #d2ae80 (never orange), hard sun #fff0d8 from high to one side, the main vehicle faded seafoam #86b5ad. Contrast: dark cracked asphalt, crisp shadows, dense green scrub, roadside posts. Default midday; at other hours keep hard sun. Heat shimmer and mirage grow with distance; dust on landings; vehicles leave the ground over crests. Grain ±2%. Reference: Paul Thomas Anderson, *One Battle After Another* (2025). Avoid: wide lenses, a washed-out frame.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Real-time 3D (three.js), super-telephoto compression, heat haze and road mirages, VistaVision fine grain

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Bob: the collar of a checked bathrobe, gold-framed sunglasses pushed onto its head, driving the lead car. The chase runs at real speed, five camera positions alternating between the telephoto's waves and the close instants; the last shot rests on Clawd's raised arm, holding the pose so the finale can pick it up.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A super-telephoto looking back at the highway from ahead of the convoy: the undulations compressed into layer upon layer of waves, three cars sinking into the waves and surfacing again, heat shimmering on the asphalt. (1.2s)
2. Cut to a camera on the bonnet: Clawd in the driver's seat of the seafoam-green convertible, sunglasses pushed on its head, both hands gripping the wheel, then looking up at the rear-view mirror. (0.8s)
3. Cut to a low tripod camera on the shoulder at the crest of a hill: the convertible flying over the crest toward the lens. (0.6s)
4. Cut back to the telephoto, this time looking forward from behind the convoy, cars vanishing and reappearing over the undulations. (1.1s)
5. Cut to a telephoto tracking shot ahead of the cars: Clawd's convertible catching air over the crest, the dark-green and grey sedans chasing up behind; after landing, Clawd stretches one hand out of the car and raises it, holding it up to the last frame. (1.3s)
