# O14 How Green Was My Valley

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Colour & War 1939–1949
- What it looks like: Opaque gouache in the 1941 original poster's gold-and-green palette: solid blocks of colour, coal-black slag heaps, heavy dark mountains in the manner of Kyffin Williams, with broken outlines. Gouache, not woodcut, to set it apart from part 3; it shows miners singing their way down the valley home.
- Style: Welsh valley gouache
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1941

## Style Prompt

Opaque gouache after Welsh painter Kyffin Williams. Directional strokes 1/80-1/40 of frame width, 2-5 times as long, ragged, fully covering; broken dark dashes outline buildings, walls and ridges; dark hills flecked with lighter green or slate knife marks. Palette: sky and light pale gold #F1C565 (not mustard), fields green #86A03F, darks coal #1D1B1E, far hills and water slate #48505E, small accents yellow #F6CF2F, other colours mixed from these; the hero the story centres on #D97757, at least 1/8 of frame height. Fields and sky light (~60%), hills and buildings dark. Warm grade under 10%, vignette, grain. Default low late-afternoon side light: fields lit, ridges rim-lit. From high ground in true perspective, a path leading the eye down; small repeated buildings (or walls, rocks) stepping along slopes. Motion: looping smoke or cloud, a lateral track. Avoid: watercolour transparency, vector fills, mud.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays young Huw: a tweed flat cap, a knotted red scarf, a daffodil. It sits on the hilltop looking over the valley first, then marches down the street in step with the singing miners. Two hard cuts: valley → pithead wheel → street.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Looking down from a daffodil bank on the hilltop: Clawd in flat cap and red scarf sitting in the left foreground, holding a daffodil, a street of terraced houses running down to the pithead and slag heaps at the valley floor; a slow pull. (1s)
2. Close-up of the pithead and its turning great wheel, terraced houses climbing the green slope behind; locked-off camera. (0.5s)
3. A lateral tracking shot along the terraced street: miners coming off shift walk down in three rows, coal dust on their faces, singing as they go; Clawd walks in the front row, houses sliding past behind it. (1s)
