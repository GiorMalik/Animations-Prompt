## Style Prompt

1980s airbrush illustration with a drafting overlay. Every shape: a hard-edged mask filled with a smooth sprayed gradient, 1–2 px overspray rim (at 1920×1080). Bright, high-key: mint #79BDA6 walls or sky, cream, neon pink #F58CB5 and magenta #C9467F accents, black-and-white checker floor or ground; purple and night under 20%; at other times of day keep mint and cream; pink carries any sunset. Figures à la Patrick Nagel (flat pale skin, few crisp lines, dark hair masses, pink and mint rim light), keeping their own age and dress. Metal and glass in Hajime Sorayama chrome: banded sky reflection, hard horizon line, cross-star glints. Overlay: thin cyan callouts #56E6FF, near-white text #D9FBFF (1/40 frame height), dotted leaders, dashed rings, dimension lines; monospace numbers count whatever the story counts, Rain Man style, as each group is circled. Slow push/pull. Avoid: synthwave suns; UI boxes; soft edges.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Raymond: a checked collar buttoned to the top, several pens and a pencil in its chest pocket. The cutting follows the counting: the first beats circle three piles of toothpicks, and when 246 appears it tilts its head, then a hard cut to Las Vegas, where each card that falls produces a counting marker. The scene plays at 1.1×.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A top-down shot of the black-and-white chequered diner floor: toothpicks scattered in piles being circled one by one, numbers rolling as they count, the camera slowly pulling back to bring the 246 at the top into the frame. (1s)
2. A hard cut to a medium close-up of Clawd: checked shirt collar, a row of pens in its chest pocket, head tilted as it looks at a toothpick pile marked 82, its head tilting lower and lower; the camera slowly pulls back. (0.8s)
3. A hard cut to the blackjack table in Las Vegas, shot over the dealer's shoulder: cards flying out of the shoe, Hi-Lo markers and a running count jumping up beside them; Clawd sitting between a Nagel-style woman and Charlie. (0.7s)
