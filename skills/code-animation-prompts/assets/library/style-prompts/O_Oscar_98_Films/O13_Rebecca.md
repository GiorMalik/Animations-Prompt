# O13 Rebecca

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Colour & War 1939–1949
- What it looks like: Charcoal drawing on rough paper: velvety black, smudged grey, moonlight and fog erased out with a rubber. Hitchcock's *Rebecca* is a black-and-white Gothic dream — the estate first in fog, then in fire — and charcoal can draw both.
- Style: Gothic charcoal drawing
- Keywords: 2D·B&W
- Year: 1940

## Style Prompt

Charcoal on toothed paper. Fine tooth (grain ~1/600 of frame width): light pressure catches only the peaks, heavy pressure fills the valleys. Grain only in midtones; velvet blacks, clean-paper lifts. Values: ~60% dark-to-mid grey, 15% deep black in the nearest foreground and largest masses, lights small. Charcoal #121111 on paper #EEECE7. Marks: broken vine lines, side-of-the-stick sweeps (1/60-1/30 of frame width) following form, hatching, finger smudges, eraser lifts for edges and glows; mist is horizontal streaks erased out of a toned area (as in William Kentridge's animated drawings). Only the hero the story centres on is in pastel (e.g. #D97757), laid fairly solid, tooth peeking through, outlined in charcoal; flames stay charcoal-and-paper. Moonlight from above; 2-3 fog bands drift at different speeds. Camera: slow subjective push, foreground parting. Avoid: digital noise, smooth digital glows, grey blacks, empty bright skies.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Vine charcoal, erasing white and finger smudging, William Kentridge, Gothic suspense

### Director's notes

Clawd plays the second Mrs de Winter: velvet hairband, cardigan, looking up at the estate. It isn't in the opening dream push; then three hard cuts: Clawd → the burning estate → Mrs Danvers. Every shot pushes inward, 2.5 seconds of pushing all the way into the fire.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A POV push along the moonlit drive overgrown with weeds; the woods part, and Manderley appears in the fog, a few windows lit that shouldn't be. (0.9s)
2. Close-up of Clawd as the second Mrs de Winter: velvet hairband, cardigan collar turned up, Manderley behind it; a slow push. (0.6s)
3. Manderley in the moonlight, the camera pushing in, fire starting to burn from window to window. (0.5s)
4. Mrs Danvers standing motionless in a burning window, hands folded, her cold face lit flat; the camera pushes toward her face. (0.5s)
