# O3 All Quiet on the Western Front

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Silent & Early Sound 1927–1938
- What it looks like: Kollwitz-style woodcut: big blocks of black, knife-cut white outlines, rough wood grain, with Clawd overprinted in a single separate orange plate. Kollwitz's 1921–22 anti-war prints come from the same war and the same grief as this film.
- Style: Kollwitz-style anti-war woodcut
- Keywords: 2D·B&W
- Year: 1929/30

## Style Prompt

Expressionist woodcut after Käthe Kollwitz, *Krieg* (1921-22). 60-70% solid black; white contour cuts 1/300-1/150 of frame width separate forms; faces are white shapes with a few black features; hands large. Figures fill the frame, cropped by its edges. Each cut tapers in and out with chipped edges, is wider the nearer it sits to the light, in slightly uneven bundles. Ink #17130F on paper #E8DFCA; texture is only wood-grain streaks and under-inked speckle (±5%). One colour block (e.g. #D97757) only on the hero the story centres on, flat on its body or clothes, cuts over it; black stays 60%+. Light enters through one opening (window, cloud gap); lamps get a short ring of cuts. The ground under figures stays uncut black. Quiet motion (breathing, a small gesture); slow pans and push-ins. Avoid: grey fog or midtones, even striped hatching, gore.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Woodcut print, Käthe Kollwitz, German Expressionism, two-plate overprint

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Paul, in a German steel helmet. The whole passage is slowed (1.2×) and held very still: it starts reaching out around beat 3, taking three beats to stretch all the way; the phantom soldiers only appear in the last shot. Four hard cuts: a face in the trench → Paul's arm → the butterfly → the cross-marked cemetery.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Panning along the trench past carved faces: one with both hands over its face, one staring in terror, one asleep. (0.7s)
2. A face under a steel helmet looking straight at the camera (a nod to the 1930 original poster); a slow push. (0.4s)
3. Mid-shot: Clawd in a German steel helmet standing on the firing step, slowly stretching its arm toward the loophole, where a white butterfly rests on a tin can along the rim; the camera pushes in. (0.9s)
4. Close-up of the white butterfly beside the loophole, Clawd's orange arm reaching in from the left; push in. (0.7s)
5. Extreme wide: no-man's-land under a flare, and a cemetery of white crosses; the column of phantom soldiers fades in and walks away into the distance; pulling back slowly. (0.6s)
