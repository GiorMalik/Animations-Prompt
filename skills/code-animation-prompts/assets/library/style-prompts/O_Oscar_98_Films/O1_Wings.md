# O1 Wings

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Silent & Early Sound 1927–1938
- What it looks like: A WWI dogfight built in three.js, graded like a 1927 orthochromatic print: washed-out sky, deep blacks, an overall amber tint, gate weave and scratches. *Wings* is most famous for strapping the camera to the aeroplane for its dogfights, so most of the cameras here are mounted on the planes.
- Style: Silent 3D dogfight · orthochromatic B&W + amber tint
- Keywords: 3D·B&W
- Year: 1927/28

## Style Prompt

Smooth-shaded 3D graded as a silent-era orthochromatic print: high contrast, reds go black. Sky near-white, ground in readable mid-greys, deep blacks (25-35% of frame) only in silhouettes. Tint: blend whites half-way toward amber #FFCC80, so the sky is pale cream-amber, never solid orange; blacks stay black. Only the hero the story centres on keeps colour, whole figure at ~70% saturation (e.g. #D97757); every lamp, fire or other light source stays grey. 24 fps gate weave (±1 px), flicker (±5%), fine grain (±4%), 1-3 thin scratches, sparse dust, rounded corners, vignette; open on a 1 s iris. Signature shot: a camera rigged to something moving with the subject, rig in frame; then chase and POV. Cut every 2-3 s; white out through cloud, smoke or glare. Reference: William Wellman, *Wings* (1927). Avoid: motion blur, colour in the greys.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

three.js 3D, orthochromatic B&W, silent-era tinting, projection wear and iris

### Director's notes

Clawd plays an American pilot: leather flying cap, goggles, white scarf, sitting in a SPAD cockpit. The opening prologue carries into the first shot; the iris opens on beat 1; then five hard cuts, about one cut every two beats. Around beat 5 it looks back and the machine guns fire; finally a white-out through cloud, handing over to the cream white at the start of part 2.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Head-on at the cockpit from the nose: Clawd in a leather flying cap, goggles pushed up on its forehead, wedged between two struts; the iris opens and the camera pushes in slowly. (0.9s)
2. Extreme wide: the squadron circling in the clouds, chequered fields and chalk trenches below, a Fokker trailing black smoke; the camera pushes in slowly. (0.6s)
3. Nose-mounted camera with engine vibration: Clawd looks back at the Fokker on its tail, raises a hand, and the twin Vickers guns flash as they fire. (0.8s)
4. Seen from the Fokker's top wing, the enemy machine gun fires, chasing Clawd's SPAD ahead; the camera is fixed to the enemy plane. (0.8s)
5. A diving shot over Clawd's shoulder: the top wing and rigging ahead, shell craters and zigzag trenches rushing toward the camera. (1.1s)
6. A SPAD in the clouds flies toward the camera like a ghost, the fog thickening until the frame turns cream white. (0.8s)
