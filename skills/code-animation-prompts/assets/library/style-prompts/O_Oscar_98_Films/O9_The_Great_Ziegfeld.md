# O9 The Great Ziegfeld

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Silent & Early Sound 1927–1938
- What it looks like: A rotating spiral 'wedding cake' stage built in three.js, in MGM silvery black and white: satin, chiffon and ostrich feathers against deep black, with only Clawd in colour. The 175-step spiral stage in 'A Pretty Girl Is Like a Melody' is this film's famous scene.
- Style: Silvery B&W 3D spiral wedding-cake stage
- Keywords: 3D·B&W
- Year: 1936

## Style Prompt

Real 3D stage spectacle in 1930s studio silver: the subject's world built as a revue set on a round tiered platform turning about 3° per second, draped and garlanded; its people become rows of costumed chorus figures with faces and arms. Costumes light satin grey, set mid-grey, pleated curtain backdrop darker, theatre deep black; whites below pure white. Soft front key; brighter rim lights edging satin and feathers near-white. Faint shafts, grain (±3%). All neutral grey except the hero the story centres on (e.g. #D97757); lamps render white. Signature shot: low medium along the tiers, figures 1/4-1/3 of frame height, the hero on the front tier. Then a 3 s curtain rise, a 10 s crane up to a level medium of the hero. Reference: Robert Z. Leonard, *The Great Ziegfeld* (1936). Avoid: flat lighting, faceless cones, fast spinning.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

three.js 3D, MGM silvery B&W, 1936 musical spectacle, ostrich feathers and chiffon

### Director's notes

Clawd is the star on top of the cake: a cluster of ostrich feathers on a rhinestone headband spilling like a fountain. The scene runs at 1.2×; the star is only revealed on the last cut, turning on beat 5, jumping, arms flung into a V. Two hard cuts linking the curtain, the crane arm and the top tier.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Low frontal camera: chiffon curtains fly up off the rotating cake stage, revealing tier after tier of fan-waving showgirls, with little Clawd at the top; the camera tilts up. (0.9s)
2. Rising along the spiral crane arm, past feather fans, a white grand piano and violinists, stopping just below the top tier. (0.8s)
3. Almost eye-level mid-shot: Clawd at the top in its ostrich-feather headdress turns to face the camera, arms flung into a V; a slow push. (0.8s)
