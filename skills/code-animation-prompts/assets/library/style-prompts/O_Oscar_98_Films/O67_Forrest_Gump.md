# O67 Forrest Gump

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Fin de Siècle 1990–1999
- What it looks like: Printed cards folded up in parallel, with white paper cores, grey board edges, shadows between the layers and shallow depth of field, set on a walnut desk. Forrest sits on a bench telling his life as a story — open a page and a chapter stands up.
- Style: Pop-up book · paper craft on a real desk
- Keywords: 2.5D
- Year: 1994

## Style Prompt

A real pop-up book filmed on a desk. Every element: a flat die-cut card on parallel folds before a 90° backdrop page, printed with transparent watercolour and grainy coloured-pencil hatching; cut edges show a white core and grey board. Default light: bright window daylight from the upper left, soft shadows between layers. Print colours (sky blue, warm sand, greens) and room light stay daylight whatever the story's time of day; that time appears only in the print (e.g. a low sun, a night sky). Walnut desk #6B3E1E; the room a warm blur. Physical depth of field, focus on the hero's layer. Signature shot: a high three-quarter view of the whole spread. One continuous move: dive into a page, pull back, turn the page instead of cutting, push into the next spread. References: Robert Sabuda, Matthew Reinhart. Avoid: solid-looking cards; dark moody rooms; cartoon faces.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Forrest: a box of chocolates in hand on the bench, a red baseball cap on the highway and on the boat. 12 beats in one unbroken shot: the feather leads the camera into the book, and two page turns (about 2.7 seconds and 4.2 seconds) stand in for the cuts. It starts running at 3.25 seconds, then stops and turns around.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. One continuous take: the closed book on the desk opens and Savannah's square stands up; the camera follows a falling white feather down to the bench, where Clawd hands the chocolates to the nurse; pulling back and turning the page, pushing in to US-163 in Monument Valley, Clawd in its red cap leading a pack of scruffy followers running closer, stopping and turning to face them; pulling back again and turning the page, pushing in to the shrimp boat 'Jenny' in a lightning storm.
