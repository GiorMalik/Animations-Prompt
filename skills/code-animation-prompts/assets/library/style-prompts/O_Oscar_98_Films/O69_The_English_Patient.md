# O69 The English Patient

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Fin de Siècle 1990–1999
- What it looks like: Watercolour sketches pasted into an old book, interleaved with maps, photographs, notes and pressed flowers, in the amber-brown of the poster overall. Almásy's Herodotus, stuffed with drawings and notes, is the whole film's frame — the camera travels through one watercolour into the Cave of Swimmers.
- Style: Explorer's scrapbook watercolour
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1996

## Style Prompt

A traveller's scrapbook, crowded edge to edge with 6–10 overlapping items, some running off frame: a taped watercolour (the main image, about 40% of frame), a hand-drawn map, a sepia photo, printed pages, handwritten notes, a pressed flower; each softly shadowed, with yellowed tape. The watercolour: wet-in-wet washes, warm ochre lights, hard-edged violet-rose shadows, pen contours, pooled edges, clear value contrast. Palette: amber paper #E8D6B0, sepia #6B4A2E, ochre #D08A3C, rose-violet #9C6A86, ink #2A1E16. Inside the painting: one warm source (torch, lamp) pooling in a dark foreground, bright sky beyond. Light amber grade on the page only, never muddying the painting; vignette about 20%, faint grain. Signature move: push into the painting until the page edges slide out. Reference: cinematographer John Seale (*The English Patient*, 1996). Avoid: smooth gradients; sparse tidy layouts; panning while a page frames the painting.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Transparent watercolour, pen notes and handwriting, scrapbook collage: tape, photographs, maps, amber-brown grade

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Almásy: a linen desert headscarf with its tail flying, a Herodotus in one hand and a torch in the other. The push into the painting is itself the transition; the two hard cuts that follow both stay in the cave — first the murals, then Clawd. Scene time is about 1.8× screen time.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. The scrapbook spread on a table, one push driving into the watercolour until the pages slide past either side of the lens: Clawd standing with its torch in the painted Cave of Swimmers. (1.2s)
2. A hard cut to a pan across the swimming figures painted on the cave wall, the torchlight flickering; Clawd is not in the frame. (0.6s)
3. A hard cut to Clawd standing at the left with its torch in one hand and the book in the other, murals beside it, dunes outside the cave mouth; the camera slowly pulls back. (0.7s)
