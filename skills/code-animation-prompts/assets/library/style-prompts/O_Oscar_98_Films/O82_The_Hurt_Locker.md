# O82 The Hurt Locker

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · New Millennium 2000–2009
- What it looks like: A page from a US Army EOD manual: even black lines, hatched shadows, stippled sand, circled numbers and leader lines, an IED exploded view, with just a touch of olive green and sand. The Hurt Locker is about procedure under pressure — the manual drawing is the procedure drawn out.
- Style: EOD field-manual line drawing
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2009

## Style Prompt

Military technical-manual illustration. Warm off-white paper #EFEADB with a faint engineering grid, uniform black ink #1B1A16 (outlines ~1/700 of frame width, hatching thinner), shading only by hatching, ground by stippling, flat tints of olive #6C6D42 and sand #D9C79C on 20–30% of the drawing; lights as ray lines, not glows. Main figure: a one-point-perspective view of the subject's setting filling most of the page; around it exploded views of the subject's key object along a dash-dot axis, circled numbers on leader lines, a parts table, WARNING and CAUTION boxes, typewriter labels (user's language) and FIG numbers, a black chapter card. Animate on twos: parts slide apart, a tool works, a figure walks. Camera: whole page, then cuts to enlarged insets. References: US Army technical manuals; Kathryn Bigelow, *The Hurt Locker* (2008). Avoid: gradient shading; too much colour; decorative fonts; sparse pages.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

### Director's notes

Clawd wears an olive-green EOD helmet with its visor up, wire cutters in hand, standing among six half-buried 155mm shells. Four shots tighten from the whole page to the cut: the long walk, the face inside the visor, Clawd's eyes, the cut. The click lands near the end of the sequence.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. The whole manual page, FIG 4-6 'THE LONG WALK': a soldier in the bomb suit walking down a Baghdad dirt road toward the camera, Clawd standing among the linked shells holding the wire cutters; the camera slowly pushes toward the street. (0.8s)
2. Close-up: the soldier in the bomb suit walking, a frowning face inside the visor; a slow push. (0.6s)
3. Close-up: Clawd in its EOD helmet with the visor up, eyes looking down toward the firing line, wire cutters raised; a slight push. (0.5s)
4. Extreme close-up: the wire cutters biting the twisted pair of firing wires above a shell — a squeeze, a click, the wire severed; the camera pushes in. (0.6s)
