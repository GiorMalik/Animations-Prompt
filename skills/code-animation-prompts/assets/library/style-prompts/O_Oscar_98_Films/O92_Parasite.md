# O92 Parasite

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Indie Era 2010–2019
- What it looks like: An architectural section cut through a Seoul hillside: the glass mansion on top, the secret bunker underground, all the way down to the flooded semi-basement, thin lines, soft material colours. Parasite is built on this up-and-down class divide — one drawing holds the whole film.
- Style: Architectural section axonometric
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2019

## Style Prompt

Vector section axonometric, low dimetric view: the cut face lies along the near-flat 7° axis, facing us, depth along the 42° axis at half scale; roof and near walls cut away to show the rooms. Cut through the subject's place (a house, a tower, a ship, a hillside). Poché on cut faces: dark concrete with aggregate, earth hatched at 45° with stones. 1 px dark-grey lines at every zoom. Default daylight under a pale sky: concrete #b3ada2, oak #d6b58a, charcoal #3e4146, pine #3f6b3e, white walls, green lawn, translucent blue water. Furnished rooms, simple scale figures. Night or other low light is a variation: multiply 30–40% moonlit blue, lit rooms painted back warm. Crane slowly framing 2–3 rooms along one continuous path; end on the whole drawing at ~80% of the frame. Avoid: 30° isometric, thick outlines, cuts between levels, a small drawing.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Ki-woo, the scholar's rock never leaving its hand: raised overhead on the lawn, hidden beside the coffee table on the stormy night, then carried all the way down the hill. Only two cuts in the whole sequence; in every shot the camera travels up and down the section, keeping rich and poor in the same frame, and the 12 beats end on the whole section.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. The glass mansion's section and the striped-lawn party in the daytime: Clawd standing at the garden gate, holding the scholar's rock overhead with both hands; the camera slowly pushes in. (0.8s)
2. Cut to the stormy night: the Park living room, Clawd hiding beside the coffee table; the camera descends like a lift, through the floor slabs and the basement, down to the bunker, the light flickering in Morse code. (1.3s)
3. Cut to a long shot following the rain downward: the camera follows Clawd, rock on its head, walking down flight after flight of long steps from the gate, through the tunnel under the road, into the low streets and the flooding semi-basement, then rising and pulling back into the whole section, the mansion at the top, the floodwater at the very bottom. (2.9s)
