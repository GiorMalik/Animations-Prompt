# O79 The Departed

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · New Millennium 2000–2009
- What it looks like: Flat multi-layer spray-paint stencils, with spray feathered edges and drips, paint eaten into the brick joints, and a torn pasted poster beside them. The Departed's last shot is a rat crossing the rail beneath the State House's golden dome, and street stencil graffiti has always loved painting rats.
- Style: Boston red-brick wall stencil graffiti
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2006

## Style Prompt

Street stencil graffiti on an old red-brick wall. Each piece is 3–5 stencils sprayed as flat colour, photo-derived in look (smallest cut detail about 1/200 of frame width): hard cut edges, soft overspray where a stencil lifted, spray dots, drips, stencil bridges. Palette: black #151312, mid grey #8C8A85, gold #C99A2E, red #C3261F; brick #7A3224, cream painted patches under the main pieces. Brick relief, mortar and rain streaks show through, so paint sinks into the joints. A busy wall: several pieces side by side, short hand-sprayed slogans about the subject (user's language), old tags, a torn halftone paste-up. Light: soft raking daylight, mild contrast, a faint swaying wire shadow; no heavy vignette. Camera: pieces spray on layer by layer, then close-ups and lateral tracks. Reference: Banksy. Avoid: clean vector edges; paint floating on the brick; too many colours; a dark wall.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Multi-layer spray stencils, Banksy's rats, red-brick wall texture, halftone paste-up poster

### Director's notes

Clawd plays the undercover cop, a flip phone pressed to its ear, and is itself one of the layers sprayed onto the wall. The first two beats spray the work on layer by layer; then the phone, the rat and Clawd hanging up arrive in fast cuts. Nobody speaks — the ringtone and the rat tell the story.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Wide: the whole wall, the stencil painting spraying on layer by layer — the city, the golden dome, the rail, the rat, Clawd with its phone, the red slogan; a slight push. (0.8s)
2. Close-up: two spray-painted flip phones ringing at each other across the frame, Clawd with a phone to its ear too; it listens, eyes flicking left; the camera drifting slowly right. (0.6s)
3. Medium shot: the rat running along the rail beneath the golden dome, 'WHO'S THE RAT?' sprayed at the upper left, red and blue police lights sweeping across the brick wall; the camera tracking right. (0.6s)
4. Close-up: Clawd putting its phone down, eyes looking right; a slight push. (0.4s)
