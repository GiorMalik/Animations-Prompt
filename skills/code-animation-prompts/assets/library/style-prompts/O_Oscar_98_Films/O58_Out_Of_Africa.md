# O58 Out of Africa

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Blockbuster Era 1980–1989
- What it looks like: Real-time 3D low-altitude flying: a chrome-yellow biplane sweeping over a soda lake, tens of thousands of flamingos startled into the air, amber hazy light. The film is most remembered for Denys taking Karen flying over Kenya, and the hand she reaches back to the rear seat.
- Style: 3D biplane over the East African Rift
- Keywords: 3D
- Year: 1985

## Style Prompt

Real-time 3D like 1980s widescreen location photography: true-scale models with fine detail (e.g. struts, wires, rivets); smooth terrain, not low-poly. Default light: late-afternoon sun low behind the hero as seen from camera, pale cream sky, amber haze, distant ridges in 3–4 paler layers, ochre ground with long soft shadows. Pale overall: about 70% of the frame in light mid-tones, shadows warm grey, not deep brown; under a fifth near-white. Grade: filmic curve, low saturation, light grain, soft vignette. Thousands of instanced birds or animals (e.g. pink #F3B3AE with crimson #DF3F55) lift or scatter in staggered waves as the hero passes. Signature shot: over the moving hero's shoulder across open land; then a high chase 10–20 m up and one shot into the sun. Reference: cinematographer David Watkin (*Out of Africa*, 1985). Avoid: cold clean CG daylight; dark sepia; lockstep flocks.

### Director's notes

Clawd sits in the front seat ahead of Karen: a leather flying cap, brass goggles, a white silk scarf. Two exterior shots hard-cut to set the speed and space over the flamingo lake; the last cockpit shot is saved for the film's gesture: Clawd turns and reaches back, and Denys takes its hand, landing on the final beats. The flying time is 1.5–1.6× screen time.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A low tracking shot from the lake surface looking up at the plane: the yellow biplane passes overhead, Clawd small in the front seat leaning out to look, flamingos rising off the water and sweeping across the lens. (0.8s)
2. A hard cut to a backlit wide facing the sun: the plane flying toward the camera, pink flocks rising on either side. (0.7s)
3. An in-plane shot from the rear seat looking forward: Clawd in its leather cap, brass goggles and wind-blown white scarf turns and reaches back, and Denys's gloved hand takes it. (1s)
