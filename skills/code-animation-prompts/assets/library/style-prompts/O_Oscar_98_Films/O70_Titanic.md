# O70 Titanic

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Fin de Siècle 1990–1999
- What it looks like: White lines on Prussian-blue blueprint paper rise up and grow perspective, then develop into a full-colour 3D ship at sunset, running on into the night. The ship itself is the film's emblem, so the shipyard drawing and the live-action scene use the same model.
- Style: Blueprint developing into 3D live action
- Keywords: 3D
- Year: 1997

## Style Prompt

A blueprint turning into photoreal-leaning 3D. Opening only (1–2 s, under 15% of the film): white edge lines about 1/800 of frame width on Prussian-blue paper (#12306B to #244D8F), hidden lines dashed, figures and terrain as contours, uneven exposure, folds, lettering. A dolly-zoom stands the elevation up into perspective; colour spreads from the hero along a soft warm edge, ghost lines fading within a second. Then the signature look: golden-hour real 3D, low sun, amber highlights #F2B860, warm shadows #3A2530, sun glitter on water, soft mist, shallow focus, gentle bloom, fine grain; then blue hour and night, warm lights coming on. One unbroken move: push in and stand up (blueprint), then, in equal shares, drift low past the hero, rise and orbit, descend. Reference: cinematographer Russell Carpenter (*Titanic*, 1997). Avoid: lingering on the drawing; hard cuts; flat CG light.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Shipyard general-arrangement blueprint, three.js ship modelled to true scale, Russell Carpenter's colour, one continuous take

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Jack, wearing a navy wool scarf. 16 beats in one unbroken shot: the blueprint finishes unfolding in 1.5 seconds, the developing finishes in 2.4, Clawd opens its arms beside Rose; sunset to night is a timelapse inside the camera move, the iceberg appearing in the last two seconds, Clawd turning to raise the alarm on the final beats.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. One continuous take: pushing toward the bow on the general-arrangement drawing; the line drawing rises into perspective and develops outward from the two figures into sunset; a low camera close to Rose's backlit silhouette, Clawd opening its arms beside her; the camera rises and circles around the port bow, the sun sinking and the ship's lights coming on; crossing the forecastle into the night, Rose coming down from the rail; then dropping behind Clawd as the iceberg emerges from the sea mist, Clawd turning and raising its arm to sound the alarm. (6.7s)
