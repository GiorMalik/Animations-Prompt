# O41 Oliver!

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Swinging Sixties 1960–1969
- What it looks like: A Victorian toy paper theatre: hand-coloured lithographed card figures left with scissor-white edges, scenery slotted in layers on sliding rods, candle footlights beneath a gilded proscenium throwing shadows onto the flats. Oliver! is a musical — perfect for a Victorian family parlour stage.
- Style: Victorian toy paper theatre
- Keywords: 2.5D
- Year: 1968

## Style Prompt

Victorian toy paper theatre (Pollock's), all printed card: black lithographic outlines about 1/400 of frame width, sparse crayon hatching on one side of each piece; muted antique hand colouring: deep red #C8262C and dull gold #E0AB36 on proscenium and curtains (never bright yellow), blue #2F5A9A for costumes; scenery, sky, water and skin in greyed browns, greys and cream card #F4ECD6. Every cut-out keeps a white scissor margin (1/200 of frame width). Flats are parallel 2.5D layers in parallax, flat within each plane. Candle footlights throw each cut-out's shadow onto the scenery behind, about 1.5× enlarged, soft, flickering. Gilt proscenium, red drapes and painted audience boxes frame a stage opening 80–90% of frame width. Camera: slow push, lateral tracking, shaky close-ups; a final pull-back shows the dark room. Avoid: bright vector colour, true 3D depth, soft studio light, figures without margins.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Pollock's toy theatre, hand-coloured lithographed paper figures, cut cards and footlight shadows, Cruikshank caricature figures

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Oliver: a paper figure on its own sliding rod, holding up an empty bowl. Wide, stage and close-up open the layers: step forward, raise the empty bowl on the beat; when Bumble roars it flinches — but the bowl stays raised.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Wide: a toy theatre on a mahogany table in a dark room, the 'Theatre Royal' proscenium, a cut-out paper figure on the table and a puppeteer's hand reaching in beside it; a slow push in. (0.5s)
2. Moving along the stage, the workhouse dining hall: Clawd as Oliver steps out from a row of children and raises its empty bowl (Please, sir…), the steaming gruel pot beside it, Bumble's brow furrowing; the camera follows it right. (0.9s)
3. Close-up of Clawd and Bumble: Bumble roars, Clawd flinches and trembles, the bowl still raised; the camera gives a small shake on the roar. (1.1s)
