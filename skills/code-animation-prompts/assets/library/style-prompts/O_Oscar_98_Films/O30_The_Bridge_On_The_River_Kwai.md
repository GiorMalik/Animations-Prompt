# O30 The Bridge on the River Kwai

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Widescreen 1950–1959
- What it looks like: A general-arrangement drawing pinned to a drawing board: white lines on Prussian blue, creases, water stains and red-pencil revisions. The film is about a colonel determined to finish his bridge, so the bridge is drawn as his engineering drawing.
- Style: Cyanotype engineering blueprint
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1957

## Style Prompt

Cyanotype engineering blueprint. The sheet fills the frame (the walnut drafting table shows only on a pull-back): mottled Prussian-blue paper #143874, uneven exposure, a greenish over-exposed margin, creases, faint water stains. White lines #ECF4FF, sharp but printed: outlines about 1/600 of frame width, hatching and dimensions half that, broken where exposure was weak, a slight cyan bloom. Draw the subject densely in drafting grammar: elevations, sections, detail circles, dimension chains, hidden lines, single-stroke lettering, a title block. Notes and actions in red pencil #D63426, in the user's language. The one hero subject is the only hand-painted element, drawn above scale (at least 1/5 of frame height), opaque gouache in one warm hue (e.g. orange #D97757); nothing else is warm. Camera: layered parallax push-in; cut to detail views. Avoid: neon glow, clean digital blue, sparse sheets.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Cyanotype print, timber trestle general arrangement, red-pencil revisions, gouache-painted hero

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Colonel Nicholson: a British army peaked cap, an officer's short cane, standing on its own bridge whistling. Three shots tell the cause and effect: the train comes onto the bridge, the plunger handle goes down, the bridge span blows apart. The explosion stays inside the drawing too: red-pencil scribbles for the fireball, hatched lines for the smoke — no real firelight drawn.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Mid-wide of the bridge elevation: Clawd (Colonel Nicholson) standing on the bridge whistling, notes floating up, prisoners marching in formation at the lower left, the plunger at the right, a train coming on from the far right; a slow push in. (1s)
2. Close-up of the plunger box (PLUNGER – ONE PUSH): the T-handle goes down, a red-pencil line running to the charges; a slight push in. (0.5s)
3. Back to the bridge elevation: the charges go off, a fireball drawn in red-pencil scribbles wrapped in smoke drawn in hatching, the bridge span and the train carriages dropping into the river; a slow pull back. (1s)
