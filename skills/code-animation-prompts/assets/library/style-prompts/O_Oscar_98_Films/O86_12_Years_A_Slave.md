# O86 12 Years a Slave

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Indie Era 2010–2019
- What it looks like: An 1850s daguerreotype in a copper-framed velvet case, developing out of blankness: a violin leaning against a chair, Clawd standing beside it with its head bowed. This is the photography of Solomon Northup's era — one object tells the film, no people drawn.
- Style: Daguerreotype developing
- Keywords: 2D·B&W
- Year: 2013

## Style Prompt

A cased 1850s daguerreotype, developing. Signature shot: the silver plate fills about 85% of the frame width inside a beaded brass mat #8a6a2c with a rounded-rectangle opening; velvet and leather only at the edges. Greyscale silver, near-black #1c1a18 to pale #ecebe6; a thin blue-violet to amber tarnish halo round the opening. Light swirls, hairline scratches, specks (±4% brightness). Soft window light from upper left; the subject poses still before a plain backdrop (an outdoor place is painted on it). One shot: the camera pushes from the open case to this framing in ~1 s, then holds; the image develops from blank mirror-grey, shadows first, a front sweeping left to right, done by ~3 s. Only the story's one hero is hand-tinted: a translucent flat wash (e.g. #D97757), shading showing through; lamps stay grey. Avoid: sepia, a small plate, fast cuts.

### Director's notes

No costume, and no people drawn: Clawd stands beside Solomon's violin, slowly bowing its head. The whole sequence has only three movements: the image developing, one slow push, the bowed head — 2.5 seconds, not a single cut.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. One continuous take: opening on the whole camera case on the table, the silver plate blank; the image developing in from one side (the violin leaning against a ladder-back chair, Clawd standing beside it), the camera slowly pushing in until the silver plate fills the frame, Clawd bowing its head. (2.5s)
