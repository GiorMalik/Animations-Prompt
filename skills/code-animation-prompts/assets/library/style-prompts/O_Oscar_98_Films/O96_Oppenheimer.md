# O96 Oppenheimer

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Right Now 2020–2025
- What it looks like: A real-time 3D reconstruction of the Trinity test site before dawn: a fireball of about 720,000 particles, quantum visions of orbits and waves, deep-blue night against orange-gold firelight, plus IMAX-style grain. Oppenheimer revolves around this one light.
- Style: 3D particle fireball · IMAX grain
- Keywords: 3D
- Year: 2023

## Style Prompt

Real-time 3D night lit by one huge particle event: a ~500k-particle fireball (or flare, eruption, wildfire) whose shape follows time: white flash (0.5 s), billowing dome by 1 s, a rolling toroid, a dust wall sweeping out. Right behind the subject like a halo, ~40% of the frame: white-gold core, bright orange-gold billows #ff9a50 with visible texture, black smoke only as thin veins and the top edge (under 15%). Its glow fills everything: warm #ffd8a0 on ground and cloud undersides, a hot rim on the subject, faces lit warm at half strength; long shadows toward camera. The rest is pre-dawn blue #2a3654 and dark earth #16120e. Clip only the core. IMAX grade: grain ±3%, warm halation, vignette, slight gate weave. Low wide angle, subject centred, slow push-ins. Reference: Christopher Nolan, *Oppenheimer* (2023). Avoid: a smooth CG ball, smoke-shrouded fire.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Real-time 3D (three.js), GPU particle simulation, IMAX 65mm print grade, Trinity test high-speed photographs

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Oppenheimer: a light-camel flat-top fedora, a smoking briar pipe in hand. Five hard-cut shots build the countdown: the tower, the device, the vision, the white flash landing exactly on the cut into the observers; the music never stops, and in the last second the fireball swells behind Clawd.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A low camera looking up before dawn at the steel tower lit by searchlights, countdown signal lights flashing, a slow push. (0.6s)
2. Cut to the bomb inside the corrugated-iron hut: a bolted sphere wrapped in cables, warm work lights; a locked-off camera. (0.4s)
3. Cut to the quantum vision on a black ground: glowing orbits around a core, interference waves below, a collapsing star. (0.4s)
4. A white flash, then the reverse shot: the observers holding welding glass, their faces lit by the fire, Clawd in its flat-top fedora with its pipe standing in the middle; a locked-off camera. (0.8s)
5. A low wide: Clawd standing in the middle of the road, the fireball rising behind it like a halo, sparks drifting through the air; a slow push. (1s)
