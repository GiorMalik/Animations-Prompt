# O34 West Side Story

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Swinging Sixties 1960–1969
- What it looks like: An Upper West Side 3D block in red-orange Technicolor dusk, saturated and stage-like: red brick, zigzag fire escapes, rooftop water towers, dancer figures that can strike poses. Restaging the film's aerial opening, the Jets' finger-snapping, and the poster's leap.
- Style: Technicolor 3D stage
- Keywords: 3D
- Year: 1961

## Style Prompt

Technicolor 3D stage set, like a 1960s studio musical. Simplified geometry, dense detail on every built surface (openings with sills, ledges, railings, ladders, strung lines); textured brick, plaster or rock, never plain. Theatrical light: a low warm sun rakes surfaces with long shadows; cool fill a quarter of the key. Painted sky of red-orange #E0481E, gold-edged cloud bands, violet only at the top edge; warm haze in the distance. The one hero subject in saturated orange #D97757, at least 1/6 of frame height, set against dark brick or given a black rim so it never melts into the sky. Puppet-like mannequin figures. Warm, saturated grade, fine grain (±3%). Signature shot: a low angle against the orange sky; then one continuous descent into the set and a ground-level push. Avoid: photoreal grime, violet-pink skies, plain walls, poses that pop.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Procedural 3D (three.js), Technicolor dusk colouring, Boris Leven sets, 1961 poster: red sky, black fire escapes

### Director's notes

Clawd slips in with the Jets, no costume — the setting tells you the film. The finger-snaps land on the drumbeat; the leap is one clean parabola, from crouch to landing bounce in a single flow. The opening aerial echoes the previous film's desk-grid; the match struck at the end sits dead centre, exactly where the next film's sunrise will be — a match cut.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A straight top-down aerial of the West Side block grid (rooftops, water towers, playground courts), descending all the way down and tilting up into the street. (1.3s)
2. A low camera at the playground edge: the Jets in a wedge formation before a graffiti wall, snapping their fingers, Clawd at the front; one continuous push circles around to a close-up of Clawd, its snap landing on the drumbeat. (1.7s)
3. Looking up from beneath the fire escape: Clawd crouches and leaps, one parabola pressed against the red sky, dancers mid-grand-jeté beside it; the camera rises with the arc. (1.2s)
4. Close-up on the fire-escape landing: Clawd strikes a match, the camera pushing in until the flame sits dead centre, then it is blown out. (0.8s)
