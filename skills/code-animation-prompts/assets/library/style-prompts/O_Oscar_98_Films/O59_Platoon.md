# O59 Platoon

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Blockbuster Era 1980–1989
- What it looks like: Fast, forceful pen lines, watercolour washes in olive green, earth yellow, smoke grey and fire orange, sketchbook paper with white margins. Like a combat artist drawing on the spot — close to this film's view of Vietnam through a foot soldier's eyes.
- Style: Vietnam combat sketch · pen and wash
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1986

## Style Prompt

Reportage pen-and-wash field sketch. Pen: warm black, fast confident lines ~1/600 of frame width, flicked ends, overshooting corners; quick hatching and zigzags for shadow, on figures and ground. Bold transparent washes with soft wet edges, blooms and uneven pigment (not flat polygons) cover about 70% of the frame, shifted 2–4 px (at 1920×1080) off the line. Colours: olive #70783E, ochre #CEA054, smoke grey #706C70, one hot accent #F0822E for sky and light sources; skin in diluted ochre; sketchbook paper #F2ECDE. Bare paper only in highlights, a thin edge band and brush gaps; light grain. A scrawled pencil note (place, time, weather) in a corner, 1/40 of frame height, in the user's language. Layer for lateral tracks; hard cuts like turning a page. Reference: US Army Vietnam Combat Artists Program (1966–70). Avoid: polished finish; empty page; muddy washes; heavy noise.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Elias: a jungle hat, dog tags. Two hard cuts from the ground to the air and back to the ground, each shot like another page in the sketchbook; cutting to the last shot, its arms immediately snap wide into the poster pose, dipping slightly as the helicopter pulls up. The scene plays at 1.1×.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Tracking the patrol advancing through the elephant grass; Clawd in its jungle hat and dog tags stands in the middle as soldiers pass it. (0.8s)
2. A hard cut to a view down from a Huey door: the burning landing zone, the grass flattened by the rotor wash and the helicopter's own shadow, Clawd small on the open ground; the camera slowly pulls back. (0.8s)
3. A hard cut to a close-up of Clawd, pulling back to the poster composition: its arms spread in a V under the orange napalm light, soldiers running past on either side, the helicopter lifting away. (0.9s)
