# O56 Terms of Endearment

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Blockbuster Era 1980–1989
- What it looks like: An open family album of faded Polaroids and Kodak snapshots: blue-shifted blacks, cream highlights, white borders. One Polaroid comes alive and becomes a tracking shot of the beach drive. Terms of Endearment is thirty years of mother and daughter.
- Style: Family album Polaroid
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1983

## Style Prompt

1970s–80s snapshots in an open album. Faded dyes: blacks lifted to teal grey about #2F3A3A, creamy rolled-off highlights, mild warm cast, slight overexposure, softness, fine grain (±4%), saturation about 60% of normal. White Polaroid borders, photo-booth strips (four frames stacked), ballpoint captions in the user's language. The album fills the frame, cream pages seen from above in soft, even light, a faint plastic sheen. In a photo, a moving subject gets a panning-shutter look (background smeared sideways, subject sharp); still subjects get shallow depth of field. People look photographed: real proportions, soft shading, slight blur, small faces. Camera: an overhead push into one photo until it fills the frame (about 3 s), a cut into the living scene as colour returns, then a pull-back. References: Polaroid SX-70, Kodacolor prints. Avoid: flat cartoon shapes, crisp modern colour, light shafts, saturated prints.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

SX-70 and Polaroid 600 prints, Kodacolor snapshots, 1970s–80s magnetic-page albums, tracking-shot motion blur

### Director's notes

Clawd plays retired astronaut Garrett, wearing gold aviator sunglasses, Aurora laughing beside it. Overall about 1.8×. Pushing into the album cuts straight into the photograph, the faded tones warming back into fresh colour; as the colour returns it throws both arms up, and the last close-up lands on the arms-wide pose.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A top-down shot of the album open on a walnut table, pushing all the way into the loose Galveston Polaroid until the photograph fills the frame. (1.2s)
2. The Polaroid comes alive: the red Corvette racing through the surf, Aurora in the passenger seat waving her hand, the colours slowly returning; the camera pulls back to a wider beach. (0.8s)
3. Close-up: Clawd as Garrett in gold aviator sunglasses stands up in the seat and opens its arms wide; a slow push. (0.5s)
