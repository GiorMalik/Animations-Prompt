# O40 In the Heat of the Night

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Swinging Sixties 1960–1969
- What it looks like: Three-colour Risograph print: deep blue, poster orange-red, fluorescent pink, with halftone dots, grain and misregistered overprint; the light is printed by using less ink. Echoing the 1967 poster's orange-red heatwave ground and black silhouettes.
- Style: Three-colour Risograph nightscape
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1967

## Style Prompt

Three-ink Risograph: orange-red #EC5032 as the dominant field (about 60%, with drip streaks), deep blue #162C76 for silhouettes and shadows, fluorescent pink #FF48B0 only for rims and halos, on warm paper #F7F2E7. Each ink is halftoned at its own angle, dots 1/300 of frame width, plates misregistered 2–4 px; grain, pinholes, slight boil. Knock orange out under blue silhouettes so they print pure blue; only the darkest accents overprint to near-black. Light is the absence of ink: beams, lamps and lit ground show bare paper, ringed in pink. Figures: blue silhouettes, pink rims; skin is 30–60% orange with a blue shadow step. At cooler times keep the red field; blue rises only into the top of the sky. Camera: handheld behind someone carrying a light, a reverse close-up, a push. Avoid: blue-dominated frames, smooth gradients, a fourth ink, clean registration.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Virgil Tibbs: white shirt collar points, a dark narrow tie, cuffs, a suitcase at its feet. The candlelight extinguished in the last film brings in the night. Three shots build the confrontation: a torch finds it, the sheriff stops chewing his gum, and it straightens its back as if to say 'They call me MISTER Tibbs!'

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. A handheld torch POV in the dark: the deputy sheriff's beam sweeps across the station platform and finds Clawd as Tibbs beside a suitcase; it looks up, eyes wide, as more torch beams switch on around it. (0.8s)
2. Reverse shot on a close-up of Sheriff Gillespie: a big peaked cap, aviator sunglasses, chewing gum — then stopping; a slow push in. (0.5s)
3. Wide of the standoff: Clawd standing between the deputy and the sheriff, torch beams crossing over it, a blue police car and the Sparta water tower behind; it straightens its back and squares its shoulders as the camera pushes in. (1.2s)
