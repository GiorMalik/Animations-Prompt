# O27 On the Waterfront

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Widescreen 1950–1959
- What it looks like: Ben Shahn's social realism: trembling transfer ink lines, with flat tempera colour blocks offset underneath, big-handed long-faced workers, colours held to grey-blue, brick red and ochre yellow. Shahn painted dockworkers and unions — exactly this film's world.
- Style: Ben Shahn social realism
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1954

## Style Prompt

Ben Shahn's social-realist tempera. Ink lines (about 1/500 of frame width) jitter and wander, break each outline into two or three strokes, sometimes doubled by a fainter offset ghost (a blotted line). Flat tempera blocks sit 2-4 px off the line, rough-edged, dry-brush streaks (one brush wide, about 1/100 of frame width) over a third of each block; model form with a darker flat of the same hue. Palette by role: slate #768492 sky and cool areas, brick #9E4834 and ochre #C89852 ground, buildings, clothes, ink #1F1A1A, warm board #F0E9DA; skin pale ochre; other colours: these darkened or greyed. The hero the story centres on wears the one warm accent on its main garment (e.g. #D97757). Big heads and hands, long faces, heavy jaws, heavy clothes. Camera: short pushes, tilt up, close-up. Avoid: smooth gradients, clean closed outlines, cheerful colour.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Terry: a dark navy knit cap, a red-and-black checked collar and cuffs, a pigeon perched on its hand. A hard cut from the scrambling hands on the dock to the quiet rooftop puts the film's two worlds side by side in two shots; in the middle shot the pigeons take flight, and the close-up rests on Clawd and the pigeon. The performance runs at 1.8×.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Mid-shot of the morning shape-up at the PIER B gate: dockworkers crowding the gate, hands raised grabbing for brass work checks, Clawd not in the frame; a gentle push. (0.8s)
2. Wide of the rooftop pigeon coop: Clawd in its knit cap with its checked collar up, holding a pigeon on its raised hand, Edie beside it, a water tower and the Manhattan skyline behind, the pigeons taking flight; the camera pushes in slowly and tilts up slightly. (1s)
3. Close-up of Clawd and the grey pigeon on its hand, a freighter, rooftops and the skyline over its shoulder; a gentle push. (0.8s)
