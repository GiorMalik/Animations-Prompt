# O74 A Beautiful Mind

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · New Millennium 2000–2009
- What it looks like: Handwritten mathematics on two surfaces: white grease pencil on the Gothic library's window glass, chalk on a blackboard, the numbers lighting up and joined by gold lines. This is how Nash sees patterns in the film: light in his mind, not a computer interface.
- Style: Window formulas and blackboard chalk + gold lines
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2001

## Style Prompt

Handwriting as the image: translucent white wax-marker strokes (about 1/300 of frame width) appear stroke by stroke on a window of square leaded panes: formulas, figures or notes about the subject, in small blocks on a quarter of the glass. Outside, the subject's world, soft-focus, mid-toned and warm (overcast gold, muted green), never brighter than the writing. A warm interior (shelves, green-shaded lamp, amber light) frames the window. At the key moment, chosen symbols brighten and soft gold lines join them into a star or polygon, lights running along. Variant: chalk on green slate with ghost writing. Palette: slate green #2F3E35, chalk white #F1EFE6, lamp amber #E0A050, soft gold #D9B45A. Camera: slow push-ins, each cut closer to the writing. Reference: cinematographer Roger Deakins (*A Beautiful Mind*, 2001). Avoid: HUD graphics; typed fonts; writing filling the glass; cold blue outside.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

White grease pencil on glass, blackboard chalk, glowing gold lines, Deakins-style window light

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Nash: an open shirt collar, a loosened knit tie, a grease pencil in hand before the window. The first shot is writing; the next two are light; every hard cut gets closer to the numbers, and at the end Clawd leaves the frame, leaving only the pattern.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Medium wide: before the Gothic library window, Clawd as Nash stands on the window seat writing game-theory formulas on the glass in white grease pencil, an autumn quadrangle outside the window; a slow push. (1s)
2. Close-up: the writing on the glass, the window beyond out of focus, Clawd's pen visible at the lower left; characters light up, and gold lines join them into a star; a slight push. (0.8s)
3. Close-up: five-digit groups of code numbers on a blackboard, the numbers lighting up one by one as a golden seven-pointed star draws itself; a slight push. No Clawd. (0.8s)
