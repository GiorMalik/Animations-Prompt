# O31 Gigi

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Widescreen 1950–1959
- What it looks like: Toulouse-Lautrec-style colour lithograph: big flat colour blocks, spattered white outlines, ink dots flicked on with a toothbrush instead of halftone, satirical caricature faces. Gigi's big moment is walking into Maxim's while all of Paris turns to stare — the Paris Lautrec painted.
- Style: Toulouse-Lautrec Belle Époque lithograph
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1958

## Style Prompt

Belle Époque colour lithograph after Toulouse-Lautrec. Flat colour plates with wobbly edges; figures and main shapes outlined in a loaded brush (blunt start, swelling middle, split dry tail), about 1/250 of frame width. Crachis only on shadow sides, cast shadows and glows: 1–3 px dots at 1920×1080, sparse, under 15% of the frame, a darker step of the colour beneath. Large surfaces get grouped dry-brush streaks. Palette: yellow #F1C232, orange-red #E0602C, olive #6E7036, black #1D1A16 on cream stone-grained paper #EFE3C3, plus a muted bottle teal and greyed mauve; flesh is orange-red thinned with paper. Caricatured profiles with drawn faces; one large figure cropped by the frame edge in front. Flat, even light; shadows are flat plates. Camera: shallow parallax, a slow pan settling on a medium shot. Avoid: Mucha halos, smooth gradients, regular halftone, all-over speckle.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Gaston: top hat, white bow tie, cane, with Gigi on its arm. The two keep walking inward, and the whole room's gaze turns with them. Three cuts on the beat: the wide of the entrance, a pan across staring faces, back to the two of them, with Gigi winking at the camera to finish.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Wide of Maxim's: Clawd as Gaston in top hat and white bow tie walks in with Gigi on its arm, cropped foreground silhouettes on either side; the nearest tables start turning to look; a slow push in drifting right. (0.8s)
2. Panning along the red velvet banquettes: one satirical caricature face after another turning to stare, lorgnettes raised, fans stopping mid-air. (0.7s)
3. Mid-shot of the two continuing inward; Gigi's gaze turns to the camera and she winks once, just like the original poster; a slight move right. (1s)
