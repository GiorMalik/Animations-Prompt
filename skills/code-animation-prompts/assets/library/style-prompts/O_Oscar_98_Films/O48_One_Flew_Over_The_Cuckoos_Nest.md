# O48 One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · New Hollywood 1970–1979
- What it looks like: Quiet realism in the manner of Edward Hopper: big flat planes of pale green wall, hard-edged parallelograms of low morning light, people kept apart from one another. Exactly the ward in One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest: fluorescent-lit order, broken by one escape.
- Style: Hopper-style American realist oil painting
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 1975

## Style Prompt

Edward Hopper-style American realist painting. Large flat planes of restrained, pale colour: institutional greens #9EB8A0 and #5E7C66, grey-green floor #7E8876, cream #ECE6D2, on visible canvas (±3%), with a few long dragged strokes about 1/80 of frame width, 5–10% off the base. Low side sun throws hard-edged parallelograms of warm light #FCE6AC on walls and ground, crossed by crisp shadows of frames or railings, indoors or out. Sky pale cream-green; skin warmed cream; clothes darkened greens. Figures painted from 3–4 overlapping soft-edged value shapes, no outline, real proportions; several people stand apart. Stillness, then one sudden event under a second: a whip pan, a burst of light. Signature shot: a still one-point interior, light on the floor. References: Edward Hopper, *Office in a Small City* (1953). Avoid: heavy outlines, vector figures, dark saturated walls, fuzzy light patches.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Edward Hopper-style realism, hard-edged slanting window light, flat oil colour blocks with drag strokes

### Director's notes

Clawd wears McMurphy's black knit cap and checked collar, playing the ending: smashing the hydrotherapy console out of the window. Screen time equals scene time: crouch and heave, lift, 1.0 seconds to throw, 1.3 seconds to hit the window, a flash and shattered glass flying toward the camera, then the cheer. A whip-pan with motion blur replaces the cut.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. One continuous take in the day room: Clawd lifts the marble hydrotherapy console overhead and hurls it, the camera whipping after it to the rear window, the glass exploding as morning light pours in, then whipping back through the light to Clawd, which throws both arms up cheering. (2.5s)
