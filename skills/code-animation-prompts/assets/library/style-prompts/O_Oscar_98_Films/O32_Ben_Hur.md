## Style Prompt

1959 65mm widescreen epic built in 3D. Default: hard high sun (about 50°), short crisp shadows, cobalt sky fading to sandy white; under a low sun shadows stay hard but long, the sky turns amber. Marble, gilt, red granite and sandstone, with mouldings and fluted columns, never bare boxes. Warm sunlit haze mixes about 20% into mid-distance colours. Technicolor-print grade: warm, saturated, slightly contrasty, no clipping or bloom; fine grain (±3%), slight gate weave. Scale is the point: monumental architecture, statues and repeated elements fill the background, never empty. The one hero subject (e.g. orange #D97757) is the most saturated thing in frame; any repeated motion (stride, wheel, oar) keeps time with speed. Signature shot: low tracking beside the moving hero, 35–50 mm full-frame; then wide, top-down, close-up, low angle. Avoid: clean CG light, empty or primitive sets, overexposure.

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Judah Ben-Hur: a linen headband across its brow, reins in one hand and whip raised in the other, driving four white horses. Five seconds, six shots, following the 1959 second unit's way of shooting: start, turn, tracking, wheels, close-up, poster composition. Every camera moves with the chariots, and the rhythm never drops.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. Wide of the starting line: nine four-horse chariots side by side, Clawd's white team among them, stands packed behind; the field surges forward as the camera slowly tightens. (0.9s)
2. High overhead of the east turn: the chariots swing around the gilded turning posts, trailing an arc of dust behind. (0.8s)
3. A parallel tracking shot right beside Clawd's chariot: the white horses at full gallop, spokes spinning, dust flying; the camera moves forward with the chariot. (1.1s)
4. A low camera between two chariots: Messala's toothed hub blades bite into Clawd's wheel, sparks flying; the camera travels with the chariot. (0.6s)
5. Close-up of Clawd on the chariot cracking the whip, Messala's black horses beside it; a slow push in. (0.8s)
6. Poster composition: a low side angle, Clawd's white team drawing level beneath the golden seated colossus on the central barrier, stands and the governor's box behind; the camera slowly widens. (0.8s)
