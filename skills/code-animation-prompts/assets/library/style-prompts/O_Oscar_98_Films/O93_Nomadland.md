# O93 Nomadland

- Category: O Oscar 98 Films · Right Now 2020–2025
- What it looks like: Chalk-pastel strokes on grey-violet paper, one wide plain and an old RV, the sky shifting from gold to rose to violet. Nomadland lives on that long, empty horizon at dusk, and the pastel makes it quiet and soft.
- Style: Magic-hour pastel drawing
- Keywords: 2D
- Year: 2020

## Style Prompt

Soft pastel on grey-violet toned paper #7d7079, tooth showing everywhere as fine speckle (±8% brightness). Sky blended smooth, a few long thin cloud bands. The land carries the texture: dry-grass ochre (gold dulled toward the paper grey) under fine upright grass strokes (1/400 of frame width wide, 1/40 long, ±15% value), dense in the foreground, thinning toward the horizon. Objects get soft diagonal hatching, broken edges. Palette by role: gold #f6cf7a at the horizon, rose #c9738a in the lower sky, violet #4b3f78 for high sky, clouds and far land, dusk blue #2b2d5c for shadows. A long horizon near mid-frame over open land or water; the subject mid-sized in the lower half. The sky drifts gold to rose to violet; lamps brighten as it darkens. One slow pan with a light push-in. Avoid: oily impasto, sharp edges, bare smooth ground.

## Supplement

### Technique keywords

Soft pastel on coloured paper, magic-hour dusk palette, paper grain texture

### Director's notes

Clawd plays Fern, no props — only walking, sitting and looking. The whole sequence is not a single cut: the walk slows to a stop on the blanket, it sits, then looks up toward the horizon; the light fades as the camping lamp and the RV doorway grow warmer.

### Storyboard from the finished film

1. One continuous wide: Clawd walking past a white old RV with its side door open, the camera tracking with it and pushing in gently, the plain and a distant lone hill sliding past; it reaches the striped blanket beside the camping lamp and sits, looking toward the horizon as the sky turns from gold to violet. (2.5s)
