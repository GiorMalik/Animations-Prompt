### Fixed rules for how to draw it

No outlines; cold cyan as the base, pink-red signs and the orange light at the end as the main lights; the further away, the more the fog eats it; reflections stretched vertically and rippled by the rain; the nearest shops have their shutters down, darkening the foreground.
