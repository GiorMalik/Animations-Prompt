### Fixed rules for how to draw it

People and cars are flat colour + one hard-edged shadow + one hard-edged highlight + a coloured outline; the background is airbrushed, with horizontal streaks for speed (signs are drawn after the streaks, so their text stays readable); the frame tilts slightly; finish with a pass of videotape colour bleed and scanlines.
