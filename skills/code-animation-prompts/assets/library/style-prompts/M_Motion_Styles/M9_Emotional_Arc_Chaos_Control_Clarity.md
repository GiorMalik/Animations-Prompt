# M9 Emotional Arc: Chaos, Control, Clarity

- Category: M Motion Styles · Mood & Atmosphere (most used on the feeling line)
- What it looks like: One arc across the whole film. It opens messy, gradually gets organized, and lands cleanly on a single sentence. Transitions morph through the same shape instead of hard cuts.
- Good for: The structure for a whole episode.

## Style Prompt

The whole film is one continuous arc: from chaos, to control, to clarity, finally landing on [the closing line]. Move between scenes by morphing the same object, the same shape — no hard cuts unless there is a special reason. The motion needs refined easing and staggered layers, with a little depth of field and light. No full-screen blur, no bouncy interfaces, no particles. Every important frame should work on its own as a poster.
