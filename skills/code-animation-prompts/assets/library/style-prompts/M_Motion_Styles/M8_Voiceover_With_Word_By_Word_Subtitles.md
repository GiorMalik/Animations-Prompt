# M8 Voiceover + Word-by-Word Subtitles

- Category: M Motion Styles · Mood & Atmosphere (most used on the feeling line)
- What it looks like: White background, one typeface; subtitles appear word by word as the words are spoken, paired with real photos, the camera drifting slowly.
- Good for: The spine of the whole film. For any video with someone explaining or talking to camera, the subtitles have to match.

## Style Prompt

The voiceover recording is the spine. Subtitles appear word by word as the words are spoken, timed from the recording with Whisper — not matched word by word to a script. White background, one typeface, black text. One slow drift per passage. Images change on the 16th notes of the music. No periods on on-screen text. Don't put a highlight-box background behind the words. The music swells on the key line and ducks automatically when the voice comes in. Loudness normalized to -14 LUFS.
