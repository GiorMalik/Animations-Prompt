# M2 Paper Handwritten Notes

- Category: M Motion Styles · Explainer
- What it looks like: A cream paper surface; marker annotations drawn stroke by stroke, handwritten words, big bold title text, and every so often the whole frame snaps to a dark background.
- Good for: Listing key points, explaining a method, "here's how you can do it" passages.

## Style Prompt

Paper-feel handwritten-notes style. Cream paper background; the palette is paper, ink black, and two accent colors only; dark mode uses charcoal gray. Marker annotations are drawn stroke by stroke; handwritten words are written out with a stroke animation (stroke-dashoffset); titles use very large bold type. A new idea every 1.5 to 2 seconds; one main action per transition. Motion needs realistic easing, squash and stretch, and follow-through. No player bars, timecodes, or other interface elements.
