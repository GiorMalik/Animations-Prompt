# M24 Four-Part Launch Film

- Category: M Motion Styles · Explainer
- What it looks like: The big picture, the pain point, the fix, the payoff — four parts, ending with a logo animation.
- Good for: Passages introducing a new tool or a skill.

## Style Prompt

You are a senior motion designer. Make a [30]-second video in 4 to 5 scenes: why this matters now, the trouble people have today, how [the tool] fixes it, and what life looks like after using it. Words appear one by one; motion uses spring physics; elements enter on the beat, never popping in out of nowhere. Pair each new element with a very soft sound effect; stay quiet the rest of the time. End with a logo animation and one line. Afterward, render it yourself and check it frame by frame; fix any overlaps, cropping, or rushed moments before showing me.
