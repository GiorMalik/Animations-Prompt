# M31 Clay Stop-Motion MV

- Category: M Motion Styles · Mood & Atmosphere (most used on the feeling line)
- What it looks like: Little clay figures moving frame by frame, with a strong handmade feel.
- Good for: Light, playful passages; your own song could become an MV like this too.

## Style Prompt

Make a [60]-second music video in clay stop-motion style: the clay figures have fingerprints and a handmade texture, and move in little jerks. Also write an original song to fit the visuals.
