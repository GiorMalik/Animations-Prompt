# M27 Dialogue-Free Short Film: Sound Tells Half the Story

- Category: M Motion Styles · Mood & Atmosphere (most used on the feeling line)
- What it looks like: No dialogue — the story is told entirely through picture and sound.
- Good for: An intro, an outro, or a short bridge between two passages.

## Style Prompt

Make a [45]-second short film with no dialogue; the sound must tell half the story. Come up with the story yourself; the theme is [theme].
