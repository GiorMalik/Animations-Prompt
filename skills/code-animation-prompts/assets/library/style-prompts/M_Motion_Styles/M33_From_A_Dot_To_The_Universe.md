# M33 From a Dot, Pull Back to the Universe

- Category: M Motion Styles · Structure & Camera (for stitching styles together)
- What it looks like: A dot on a black screen slowly opens into a scene, then keeps pulling back until the whole desktop is a dot in space, then pushes into another scene.
- Good for: Talking about scale: "from one small tool to a whole system".

## Style Prompt

Start from a dot on a black screen, open out into [a scene]; the camera pushes and pulls across several scales, pulling back until the whole frame is a dot in space, then pushes into [another scene], and finally lands on [one line].
