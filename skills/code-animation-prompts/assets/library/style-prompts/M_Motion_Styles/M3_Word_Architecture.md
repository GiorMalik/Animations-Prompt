# M3 Word Architecture

- Category: M Motion Styles · Words & Pull-Quotes
- What it looks like: Words only. Every word has a "structural role": some are beams, some are pillars, some are floors. The earlier words build something up, and the final sentence "stands" on what they built.
- Good for: The closing line: "Learn. Believe in yourself." The most worthwhile passage in the whole film.

## Style Prompt

Make a [20]-second voiceover animation using words only. The lines, in order, are: [line 1], [line 2]… Every keyword has a structural role — for example, "learn" is the foundation, "believe" is a pillar, "yourself" is the person standing on top. Build the words out of their real letterforms, and let each line change the structure of the frame. The last line must "stand" on what the earlier words built. Colors: dark background, paper-colored words, one accent color. No clichéd shots of microphones, crowds, or flags; no stock video. Afterward, pick 8 frames to check: the reading order is right, and the final platform really is built from the earlier elements.
