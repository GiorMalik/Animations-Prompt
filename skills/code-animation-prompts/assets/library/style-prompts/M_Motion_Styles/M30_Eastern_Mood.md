# M30 Eastern Mood

- Category: M Motion Styles · Mood & Atmosphere (most used on the feeling line)
- What it looks like: Scenes from classical poetry — a moonlit night, river waters, a pavilion, for example — optionally paired with an interactive web page.
- Good for: Passages about a state of mind or about culture; also works as an intro.

## Style Prompt

Based on what you know about me, pick a classical Eastern mood I might like (a moonlit night, river waters, a pavilion, for example), make a [30]-second video, and also make an interactive web version.
