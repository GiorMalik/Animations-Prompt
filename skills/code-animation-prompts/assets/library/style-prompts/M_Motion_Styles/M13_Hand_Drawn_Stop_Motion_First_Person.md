# M13 Hand-Drawn Stop Motion, First Person

- Category: M Motion Styles · Mood & Atmosphere (most used on the feeling line)
- What it looks like: Hand-drawn stop motion, a little shaky frame by frame, where the things on screen speak for themselves.
- Good for: Letting a thing speak for you. For example, letting "that little announcement-grabbing tool" introduce itself.

## Style Prompt

Make a hand-drawn stop-motion animation. You are [the thing]; introduce yourself to the audience in the first person.
