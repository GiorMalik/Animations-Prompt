# M5 One Continuous Shot

- Category: M Motion Styles · Structure & Camera (for stitching styles together)
- What it looks like: No cuts from start to finish; every scene "grows" out of the one before it.
- Good for: Passages about change — for example, "you used to push it yourself, now you sit in it and it drives itself" — as one shot turns into another.

## Style Prompt

One continuous shot. Every scene grows out of the previous one: words rise out of a line, a button unfolds into a page, a single color floods the frame to carry us into the next passage. No fades, no blur, no hard cuts. One eased camera move per scene.
