# M10 Turning an Abstract Concept or Feeling into Imagery

- Category: M Motion Styles · Mood & Atmosphere (most used on the feeling line)
- What it looks like: No specific people or objects — a feeling is expressed through changing shapes, words, and colors.
- Good for: Passages about fear, anxiety, unease, "overthinking", and feelings like that.

## Style Prompt

Make a [15]-second motion graphic expressing the feeling of "[emotion/concept]" — for example "anxiety" or "fear of the unknown". No specific people or objects: express it through changing shapes, words, and colors. Move slowly from [one state] to [another state].
