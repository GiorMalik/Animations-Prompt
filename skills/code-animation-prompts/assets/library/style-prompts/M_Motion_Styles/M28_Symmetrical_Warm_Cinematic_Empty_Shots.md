# M28 Symmetrical, Warm, Cinematic Empty Shots

- Category: M Motion Styles · Mood & Atmosphere (most used on the feeling line)
- What it looks like: A left-right symmetrical frame in soft warm pinks, like a delicate little stage. For example, the four seasons passing outside a train window, a cup of coffee on the table.
- Good for: Passages about time passing, about stopping to think. For example, "this time away from posting".

## Style Prompt

The four seasons flow past outside a train window; the carriage is warm, a cup of coffee on the table. The frame is left-right symmetrical, soft warm pinks, like a delicate stage set, the camera very steady.
